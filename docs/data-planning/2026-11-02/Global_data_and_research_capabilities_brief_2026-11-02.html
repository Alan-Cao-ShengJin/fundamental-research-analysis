Research infrastructure brief · 2 November 2026

Worldwide data for stronger assumptions

A shared brief for research and data-engineering teams: source families to collect, the assumptions they can support, and the research capabilities we still need to strengthen.

Global coverage is the design requirement. A stock's listing country is not its demand, production, funding or input-cost geography. Build common schemas with series-level country coverage, then add regional sources where global datasets are incomplete.

What general data can do: establish market size, cycle, pricing, costs, capacity, funding and plausible ranges. Company forecasts still need an issuer baseline, exposure mix and a justified mechanism for share, realization, costs and capital. A pipeline does not supply that reasoning automatically.

Public company disclosures remain a common, reusable data source across the universe; they are different from proprietary company telemetry. We should collect the available filings, segment figures and calls consistently while accepting that some foundational inputs remain estimates.

Source documentation and product/release pages were checked for this plan. New API retrieval, historical coverage, bulk permission and internal-use rights remain implementation checks. The plan creates no feeds or subscriptions.

What to build first

0

Global identity, source registry and reproducibility

GLEIF/OpenFIGI where useful, worldwide official-source/NSDP registry, market/currency calendars, identifiers, raw-vintage archive and observation contract.

Proof: A restatement and delayed release can be replayed correctly at a frozen cutoff; country/source gaps remain visible.

1

Shared macro, trade, cost and public-disclosure backbone

World Bank, IMF, BIS, OECD, UN Comtrade/WTO, UNIDO, ILO and commodity prices, plus country extensions and a reusable public filings/peer panel.

Proof: Choose series by covered business exposure, not by the exchange where the stock is listed. Demonstrate units, definitions and publication/revision histories.

2

Sector observation modules

Add matched units/price/capacity/input datasets for the sectors actually entering research: software/hardware/chips, financials, consumer, industrials, health, energy, property and transport.

Proof: For each module, map observations to foundational drivers, identify what remains issuer-specific and reject at least one misleading proxy.

3

Targeted licensed gap-filling

Trial one adequate global fundamentals/point-in-time estimates route and only decision-relevant sector products. Use existing entitlements where rights and delivery are sufficient.

Proof: Compare sample coverage, vintages, definitions and incremental assumption usefulness before committing to a recurring product. This is not a shopping list.

Parallel

Research-method work owned by us

Assumption calibration, full sector archetypes, independent economic review and executed evaluations first; then cross-country bridges, scenarios and case learning.

Proof: A real research exercise demonstrates the method and an inspectable model, rather than merely adding another instruction file.

Division of work: Data engineering owns acquisition, normalization, vintage preservation, queries and feed health. Research owns relevance, exposure mapping, calibration, assumptions, models and economic checks. Analyst review supplies judgment and acceptance feedback. Collection priorities and licenses should follow concrete research needs.

How observations become an assumption

  1. Match the population: product, customer/end market, geography, fiscal period, currency and accounting basis.
  2. Define the mechanism: demand, price/mix, delivery, input cost, capacity, capital or funding; separate direct evidence from context.
  3. Calibrate where possible: examine past relationships, seasonality, procurement/recognition lags and structural breaks, using chronological holdouts rather than tuning on the outcome. Do not force a regression onto thin or incomparable data.
  4. Make the judgment explicit: choose the central input and useful range, identify contradictory evidence, and show what remains issuer-specific or unknown.
  5. Propagate and review: aggregate the foundational pieces into statements and value; test sensitivities, cash/funding and subsequent matched actuals.

For example, equipment units can be built from a matched addressable unit market × company share, then revenue from units × realized net price. Global capex growth informs the market assumption; it does not establish company share, acceptance timing or margin. Commodity prices inform procurement scenarios after grade, geography, currency, contract and inventory-lag adjustments.

Apply evidence in layers: issuer/peer disclosed operating facts; matched sector observations; broader sector-cycle indicators; macro context. A weaker layer can support a range or stress, without being presented as a directly measured company input.

Do not fill these gaps mechanically: market share gains, customer churn/retention, net take rates, conversion, mine grade, factory yield, underwriting quality, actual net drug prices, backlog convertibility and management intent. These need direct disclosures, careful comparative evidence or explicit judgment.

All twenty sector requirements

Choose the relevant economic activities, including more than one for material mixed businesses. Every source below is defined in the catalogue; country coverage must match the operation.

Software / SaaS

Collect: IT budgets; software price/labor indices; business formation/capex; public peer retention and sales-efficiency definitions.

Sources: Gartner worldwide IT-spending estimates/forecasts, World Bank WDI and related indicator datasets, OECD Data Explorer, ILOSTAT labor indicators, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, Official disclosure and regulator network, Standardized global fundamentals / as-reported panels.

Build: Customer cohorts and usage x retention/expansion x realized price, constrained by sales capacity and cash investment.

Still issuer-specific: NRR/churn, contract timing, sales productivity, cloud commitments and product mix remain issuer-specific disclosed/judgment inputs.

Common error: Equating global IT spending growth with one company's ARR or revenue; treating provider forecasts as actual spending.

Semiconductors

Collect: product/region sales and units; equipment/fab/wafer activity; device end demand; inventory and trade; input/labor costs.

Sources: WSTS / SIA semiconductor market data, SEMI Market Intelligence / WWSEMS, IDC global device and infrastructure trackers, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, UNIDO industrial production and manufacturing trade, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: End-market units x chip content/share, or wafer starts x yield x net wafer price; build capacity, cost and investment separately.

Still issuer-specific: Process/node yields, customer exposure, utilization and contract pricing need issuer/peer evidence and supported estimates.

Common error: Using a smoothed industry sales index as a single month; applying memory prices to logic or foundry pricing.

Hardware / devices / contract manufacturing

Collect: device shipments; replacement mix; geographic demand; electronics orders/trade; components; freight; wages.

Sources: IDC global device and infrastructure trackers, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, UNIDO industrial production and manufacturing trade, Taiwan MOEA electronics/manufacturing statistics, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, Drewry World Container Index and shipping research, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Units by product/geography x net ASP; BOM and conversion cost, inventory/working capital and capacity investment.

Still issuer-specific: BOM content, customer allocations, channel inventory and company product/region mix are not supplied by global aggregates.

Common error: Treating shipment growth as sell-through or average export unit value as a clean like-for-like ASP.

Internet platforms / ecommerce / ads

Collect: retail/payment market; web/app activity; advertising spend; digital adoption; public peer monetization.

Sources: National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, BIS CPMI payment statistics, Similarweb web/app datasets, Sensor Tower mobile/digital intelligence, Cloudflare Radar, WARC global advertising data and forecast vintages, ITU DataHub ICT indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Transactions/GMV x net take rate, or monetizable impressions x realized CPM, plus fulfillment/subsidy and investment economics.

Still issuer-specific: Conversion, net take, incentive incidence, paid-user cohorts and profitability require issuer evidence.

Common error: Treating visits/downloads as paid demand or GMV as revenue; combining gross and net ad/transaction bases.

Media / gaming / content

Collect: country/platform audiences; release and content supply; app spend/activity; ad budgets; consumer income.

Sources: Sensor Tower mobile/digital intelligence, Ampere Analysis media and games research, WARC global advertising data and forecast vintages, World Bank WDI and related indicator datasets, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Title/release and payer cohorts x monetization/retention, or subscribers x net price; content cash and amortization schedules.

Still issuer-specific: Title success, revenue share, content budgets, rights, deferred revenue and franchise durability still need direct evidence and ranges.

Common error: Mistaking store consumer spending for publisher net bookings or content cash spending for current-period expense.

Banks

Collect: credit/deposit balances; rates and curves; sector NIM; delinquencies/NPL; capital; property and funding conditions.

Sources: BIS credit, banking, property and FX statistics, IMF topic datasets and WEO vintages, IMF Financial Soundness Indicators, National banking/insurance regulatory statistics network, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Average earning assets x yield less average funding x cost; credit cohorts, fees, costs, RWA and distributable capital.

Still issuer-specific: Loan/deposit mix, repricing, credit underwriting, concentration, capital rules and management provisions require institution-level evidence.

Common error: Using a generic net-debt enterprise-value bridge, or applying a national credit/NPL average directly to one bank.

Insurance / reinsurance

Collect: premium markets; claims inflation; rates and asset returns; weather/catastrophe exposure; sector solvency.

Sources: Swiss Re sigma insurance research/explorer, IMF Financial Soundness Indicators, National banking/insurance regulatory statistics network, IMF topic datasets and WEO vintages, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Copernicus ERA5 climate reanalysis, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, WHO health indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Exposure x net premium rate -> earned premiums; claims frequency/severity/reserves, investment and regulatory capital.

Still issuer-specific: Loss development, guarantees, surrender behavior, reinsurance, IFRS/statutory bridges and remittance need issuer schedules.

Common error: Assuming premium growth proves underwriting profit or that reserve releases represent repeatable operating improvement.

Payments / fintech

Collect: payment volumes and values; cashless adoption; consumer/business spend; funding and credit conditions.

Sources: BIS CPMI payment statistics, World Bank WDI and related indicator datasets, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, BIS credit, banking, property and FX statistics, IMF Financial Soundness Indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Matched eligible transactions/value x net take, with incentives/network costs; funded lending and loss cohorts where relevant.

Still issuer-specific: Net economics, routing, merchant mix, cohort losses and funding terms cannot be inferred from total payment values.

Common error: Counting wholesale payment flows as merchant spending or applying volume growth to revenue without a take-rate bridge.

Brokers / exchanges / asset managers

Collect: turnover and product volumes; IPO/issuance; fund assets/flows; market returns; interest rates.

Sources: World Federation of Exchanges statistics, ICI / IIFA fund assets and flows, EPFR global fund flows and allocations, Exchange-specific statistics; HKEX example, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, BIS credit, banking, property and FX statistics, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Average AUM x net fee, trading/clearing volume x realized fees, plus funded cash balances and associated expenses.

Still issuer-specific: Mandate mix, performance fees, distribution rebates and capital/interest exposure need company disclosures.

Common error: Equating AUM changes with flows or exchange turnover with net capital buying.

Real estate / REITs

Collect: location/type rents; vacancy; absorption; new supply; property yields/transactions; rates; construction costs.

Sources: JLL global and city-level property research, MSCI Real Assets / Real Estate data, National/city property, claims and transport adapters, IMF topic datasets and WEO vintages, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Area/rooms x occupancy x net rent/rate, lease expiries and costs -> cash NOI/AFFO; funding/capex and NAV separately.

Still issuer-specific: Lease schedules, property mix, incentives, maintenance capex and debt terms remain company/asset-specific.

Common error: Using a global office average for a local logistics portfolio, or treating book NAV as automatically realizable owner value.

Pharma / biotech

Collect: disease population; trial milestones/results; country approvals; competition; drug units/net-price evidence; patent research.

Sources: WHO health indicators, ClinicalTrials.gov study registry, WHO ICTRP international trial registry network, Medicine/device regulator network; openFDA example, IQVIA MIDAS and related market measurement, Citeline clinical and drug intelligence, EPO OPS / WIPO PATENTSCOPE, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Eligible treated population x adherence/duration x net realized price; timed clinical probability tree, costs and commercial rights.

Still issuer-specific: Asset-specific clinical interpretation, actual net rebates, exclusivity, funding/runway and licensing terms require deeper analysis.

Common error: Multiplying peak sales by one success probability without timing/costs; treating registration or patent publication as proof of efficacy/exclusivity.

Healthcare services / devices / CRO-CDMO

Collect: health spending; procedures; reimbursement/payer context; installed capacity; trial activity; device trade.

Sources: WHO Global Health Expenditure Database, WHO health indicators, OECD Data Explorer, National/city property, claims and transport adapters, ClinicalTrials.gov study registry, WHO ICTRP international trial registry network, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Procedures/members/installed consumables or executable contract work x net price; staffing, utilization, collections and capacity investment.

Still issuer-specific: Provider mix, payer rates, collections, cancellations, executable backlog and capacity constraints need company-level evidence.

Common error: Using registered trial counts as CRO revenue or national healthcare expenditure growth as a provider's same-store growth.

Consumer / retail / brands

Collect: retail volume/value; income; CPI; category/scan data; agricultural inputs; wages; tourism; weather.

Sources: World Bank WDI and related indicator datasets, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, OECD Data Explorer, NielsenIQ retail measurement, Euromonitor Passport, FAOSTAT production, trade and food/agriculture data, FAO Food Price Index and food-price monitoring, International Coffee Organization, ILOSTAT labor indicators, UN Tourism indicators, Copernicus ERA5 climate reanalysis, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Stores/cohorts or category units x share x net price/mix; promotions, procurement, logistics and funded working capital.

Still issuer-specific: Same-store traffic/ticket, franchise economics, actual procurement/hedging and channel inventory remain issuer-specific.

Common error: Treating nominal category sales as volume or a commodity price change as immediate, full company margin pass-through.

Industrials / equipment / engineered projects

Collect: manufacturing production/orders; trade; equipment markets; automation; end-customer capex; materials; wages; freight.

Sources: UNIDO industrial production and manufacturing trade, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, WTO trade, tariffs and barometers, S&P Global PMI and sector surveys, Off-Highway Research equipment-market data, IFR World Robotics, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Drewry World Container Index and shipping research, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Market demand/share or executable orders -> deliveries/acceptance x net price; matched costs, installed-base service and capital.

Still issuer-specific: Firm orders, delivery schedules, cost-to-complete, customer funding and service attachment need issuer evidence.

Common error: Applying broad capex/production growth to every machinery segment, or treating orders/backlog as cash or guaranteed sales.

Autos / EVs / components / batteries

Collect: production; retail registrations; powertrain; trade; EV adoption; installed battery usage; material prices; rates.

Sources: OICA motor-vehicle statistics, Regional vehicle registration/industry association network, IEA Global EV Data Explorer, SNE Research battery usage, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, Fastmarkets commodity assessments, IMF topic datasets and WEO vintages, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Retail/wholesale bridge by product/region x net unit price; content/share for components, cost/utilization and finance exposure.

Still issuer-specific: Product rollout, discounts, channel inventory, OEM allocation, factory yield and procurement remain company-specific.

Common error: Adding production, wholesale and registrations together; equating battery usage with cell shipments or storage-system demand.

Oil / gas / refining / energy infrastructure

Collect: physical supply/demand; inventories; refining; trade; reference prices; capacity; regional differentials.

Sources: JODI Oil and Gas World Databases, EIA energy data including international series, IEA energy datasets, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Production/depletion or throughput x realized netback; extraction/refining costs, sustaining capex and contracted cash flows.

Still issuer-specific: Field decline, project timing, refinery slate, contracts, hedges and actual operating costs need asset/company evidence.

Common error: Using one benchmark for every realized price or confusing gross margin with cash after sustaining investment.

Utilities / power / renewable projects

Collect: power load; generation by fuel; capacity; weather; fuel prices; outages; tariff/regulatory and funding context.

Sources: Ember electricity datasets, IRENA capacity, generation and cost statistics, IEA energy datasets, Copernicus ERA5 climate reanalysis, ENTSO-E European electricity transparency, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, Global prices, actions, calendars and FX reference service, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Available capacity x output/capacity factor x net tariff/PPA, or regulated rate base x allowed return; capex and funding constraints.

Still issuer-specific: Asset location, curtailment, contracts, local regulation, project budgets and debt/equity funding remain company-specific.

Common error: Turning capacity additions directly into earnings or using national generation averages for a differently located asset fleet.

Metals / mining

Collect: commodity benchmarks; global mine/refined supply; steel demand; inventories; trade; energy and labor inputs.

Sources: USGS global mineral statistics, worldsteel steel production and industry data, World Bank commodity prices / Pink Sheet, LME reference prices, inventories and market data, Fastmarkets commodity assessments, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, EIA energy data including international series, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Ore x grade x recovery x payability x realized netback; mine life, costs, sustaining/growth capex and closure obligations.

Still issuer-specific: Mine grade, recoveries, life, sustaining capex and contracts require technical reports/issuer evidence.

Common error: Inferring mine costs/grades from global metal prices, or subtracting sustaining capex twice after a cash-cost metric that already includes it.

Telecom / towers

Collect: penetration; connections; data adoption; price baskets; technology mix; devices; wages; country/spectrum context.

Sources: ITU DataHub ICT indicators, GSMA Intelligence, IDC global device and infrastructure trackers, World Bank WDI and related indicator datasets, National statistics and central-bank adapters via NSDP, ILOSTAT labor indicators, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Active connection cohorts x realized ARPU, or leased sites x tenancy x rent; network capex, spectrum and lease/funding cash.

Still issuer-specific: Churn, billing mix, actual network costs, spectrum payments and lease obligations need issuer/local evidence.

Common error: Treating subscriptions as people or a standardized country price basket as operator net ARPU.

Airlines / shipping / rail / logistics / airports

Collect: traffic/capacity; load factors; freight/yields; port activity; network/fleet capacity; fuel; trade; travel.

Sources: IATA air passenger/cargo statistics, UNCTAD maritime and trade indicators, IMF PortWatch, Baltic Exchange freight indices, Drewry World Container Index and shipping research, UN Tourism indicators, CoStar / STR hotel benchmarking, JODI Oil and Gas World Databases, EIA energy data including international series, UN Comtrade goods-trade datasets, National/city property, claims and transport adapters, Official disclosure and regulator network.

Build: Capacity x load x unit yield or volume x net price; fuel/handling/labor/lease cost and committed capacity investment.

Still issuer-specific: Network mix, contracts, ancillary revenues, hedges, fleet commitments and receivables need issuer/asset evidence.

Common error: Multiplying an already revenue-per-capacity measure by load factor again, or mixing daily spot freight with contracted realized revenue.

Documentation and retrieval limits

No new API retrieval or bulk-rights trial was performed. Multi-country networks are requirements with example sources, not completed global adapters.

  • FactSet Estimates / LSEG I/B/E/S: FactSet product-catalog URLs resolved but their client-rendered content was not readable in the text browser. Entitlement, schema and usable history need a sample trial. LSEG point-in-time capability is described in its linked product material.

  • National/city property, claims and transport adapters: The RVD example page was not retrievable through the review browser; The data engineer should confirm the current official route. URA redirects to its current property-data page.

Acceptance checks before scale

  • Replay an as-of query that excludes later releases and later revisions.
  • Retain nulls/missingness without converting them to zero or silently forward-filling an essential gap.
  • Preserve and reconcile stock/flow, cumulative/standalone, monthly/annual and nominal/real distinctions.
  • Keep actuals, derived values, provider estimates and provider forecasts in separate statuses.
  • Handle changing industry/HS codes, country boundaries, sources, sample coverage and definitions explicitly.
  • Demonstrate matched source-to-normalized lineage and source rights for one selected series before scale-up.
  • Show exact geography and operating exposure; never equate listing country with operating market.
  • Link a sample observation through a supported assumption and calculation to affected outputs, without asserting that lineage proves causality.

Seek enough useful history to observe the relevant cycle and breaks; there is no fixed year count that makes a weak proxy valid. Preserve actual publication and retrieval times; unknown first-availability remains unknown. Do not manufacture old vintages from today's revised history.
