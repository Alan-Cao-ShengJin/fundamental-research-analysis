# Empirical comparisons and validation of public-source research

Read when building historical analogues, validating a completed company study or reviewing subsequent outcomes. These procedures support judgment under uncertainty; they do not require proprietary data, a minimum case quota or statistical certainty before forming a call. Specifications and populated records must remain distinguishable.

## Build a reusable historical comparison library

Start with a decision-relevant question: margin recovery after restructuring, capacity expansion returns, acquisition integration, guidance reliability, customer retention or another economic mechanism. Define the comparison universe and selection rule before selecting memorable successes. Include failed, abandoned, impaired and delisted cases where discoverable and relevant. Record coverage gaps and exclusions; a collection found opportunistically is not necessarily representative.

Use original publicly available filings and dated disclosures. Preserve the starting information set, later outcomes and source vintages separately. At a historical forecast date, do not import later-restated facts or knowledge of the result. Current research may learn from older completed cases available today; this does not make those outcomes available to an earlier backtest.

Store each case in a queryable structured artifact with the following fields, adapting to the question:

| Field group | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and selection | Case/entity/episode IDs, inclusion rule, universe/coverage, selection date and reason for exclusions |
| Starting conditions | Decision date, industry, lifecycle, geography, cycle/rate regime, leverage, accounting/perimeter and relevant business economics |
| Original expectation | Dated guidance/forecast or an explicitly labeled reconstruction, metric definition, source locator and known availability |
| Action and investment | Capital committed, price paid where relevant, operating milestones and expected timing |
| Outcome | Matched actual metric, measurement date/horizon, capital/cash outcome and source; unresolved or incomplete outcomes remain labeled |
| Attribution and comparability | Execution, external conditions, accounting/perimeter effects, confounders, transferable mechanism and limitations |
| Research use | Assumption/model IDs informed, inference/range, contrary cases and review date/version |

Use distributions, ranges and failure modes where coverage supports them. With few cases, use them as analogies and expose selection limits instead of inventing a precise probability. Weight comparability by mechanism and starting conditions, not the convenience of the outcome. Separate business delivery from share returns; measure investment outcomes using the actual starting valuation, distributions, FX, horizon and benchmark when data permit.

Keep the library reusable and outside active skill instructions. Company facts and historical outcomes belong in referenced research artifacts, not generic SKILL.md examples. A schema, empty directory or a few selected anecdotes is not a populated empirical track record.

## Verify pivotal claims against originals

Prioritize claims that drive the call or expose material downside, plus high-risk derived calculations. For each, retain claim ID, exact source/locator and vintage, source text/table value with qualifications, units/period/perimeter, transformation, model dependency, result and unresolved issue. Use statuses such as verified as stated, corrected, attributed forecast, reasoned inference or unavailable. Source verification establishes what a document says; it does not make management's future claim true.

Open the original document and surrounding notes, not only a search snippet or generated summary. Check issuer/share-class identity, amendments, currency/scale, actual versus guidance, reported versus adjusted, annual versus interim, and cumulative versus standalone period. Reproduce material derived values from the underlying observations. Ensure the citation supports the specific claim rather than a general company description. Retain contradictions instead of smoothing them away.

If the original is inaccessible, label the intermediary and limitation and seek a public alternative where useful. A URL, source ID, content hash or checked box alone does not prove the assertion. Do not present an inaccessible source as read. An educated estimate can still support a call when its rationale and uncertainty are explicit.

## Review calculations and economics separately

Reproduce pivotal arithmetic using the executable model or a separate calculation: segment/per-share conversions, cash bridges, valuation claims, return components and scenario consequences. Where material, perturb a key input and verify dependent outputs and quoted prose update. Inspect an explicit adverse case and a case with an unchanged multiple/discount. Reconciliation verifies arithmetic, not the evidence behind the assumptions.

For the economic review, start with original evidence and reconstruct the core mechanism before reading the finished recommendation where practical. Test a credible competing explanation, the strongest disconfirming observations, competitor response, reinvestment returns, advantage duration, priced expectations and odds versus payoff. Re-reading the original conclusion or repeating its assumptions is not independent validation. This procedure does not require additional agents or authorize delegation.

Record review scope, artifacts/versions inspected, calculations actually repeated, findings/corrections, remaining disagreements and consequences for the call. If no separate reviewer is available, describe a second self-review accurately. Never claim a blind or independent review that did not occur. Resolve a material correction by rerunning affected models/valuations and updating dependent wording; a softer sentence alone is not resolution.

## Demonstrate behavior and learn from outcomes

Use realistic public-source exercises or clearly labeled synthetic fixtures to test specific failure modes. Verify observable behavior: rejects an irrelevant citation, preserves a source correction, distinguishes growth from value creation, avoids unsupported perpetual margins, includes failed analogues and assesses a high-win-rate poor-payoff case correctly. Structural validators test metadata, links and artifact shapes, not these analyst judgments.

Freeze each published forecast, assumptions, source cutoff, target horizon and revision conditions. On matched subsequent disclosures, compare actuals with the original estimate before evaluating the revision. Attribute differences to operating drivers, investment/cash, data/accounting/perimeter, capital allocation and valuation assumptions where identifiable. Track losses and rejected theses as well as successes. Do not judge process quality solely by a winning share price or a single forecast error.

For probabilistic claims, define the success event and horizon in advance and assess calibration over enough comparable observations; do not claim skill from one outcome. Preserve unobservable and unresolved results. Learn from repeated evidence without tuning on the same evaluation set and declaring independent success. A future evaluation plan or saved watch is not a running service or demonstrated research improvement.
