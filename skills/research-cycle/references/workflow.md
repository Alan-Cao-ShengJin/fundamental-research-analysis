# Workflow, routing and feedback

## Work in decision-sized units

For a full company case/update, use the [coverage plan](research-coverage.md) to account for every specialist and the selected catalog sequence. Conditional work enters at its point of decision value and sends findings back through affected dependencies. Reconcile the plan before declaring completion; a generated plan is not completed analysis.

Start with a research brief: question, objective, horizon/deadline, information cutoff, existing work, available sources/tools and required deliverable. For portfolio or implementation work, also record mandate/version, permitted instruments and exposures, capital/liquidity needs, positions in scope and actual/paper mode. Do not infer missing constraints from account balances or a return aspiration.

The approved mandate is owned by the application. A research brief may propose a change, but cannot make it effective. Personal loss capacity is neither an expected return nor an instruction to consume the full loss budget.

## Choose initiation or the appropriate update

Across company modes, use [investment conviction](../../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) to establish or retrieve the original decisive proposition before new commentary displaces it. Test that proposition and active avoidance conditions against relevant evidence and original milestones before the final conclusion. Separate belief, forecast/value, price-based rating, eligibility and portfolio effects; price volatility alone does not settle any factual premise.

Apply [request routing](request-routing.md) first. First coverage or an explicitly fresh study uses [stock initiation](../../stock-initiation/SKILL.md) and the complete path below. Existing coverage uses [model revision](../../model-revision/SKILL.md) in one of two modes: regular review, preserving operating forecasts unless concrete evidence supports change; or major earnings/catalyst update, evaluating frozen assumptions against disclosures before recalibrating affected forecasts. Carry unaffected artifacts by reference rather than restarting historical construction or the full business investigation.

Revision can end with no material change or identify an invalid baseline requiring targeted reconstruction. Major perimeter, accounting, governance or thesis changes can reopen broader stages. The shared standards remain; the evidence scope and skill emphasis differ.

## Complete company-initiation path

Apply the [shared materiality principle](underwriting-standard.md#integrate-the-analysis-by-materiality): the steps below express dependencies, not fixed analytical weights. Market context, governance and operating evidence can develop together, and a later-discussed technique does not displace the existing framework.

Build the model before valuation and reach the conclusion after validation. The [company sequence](company-sequence.md) defines stages and readiness gates.

1. Scope the question; gather filings, existing model, trackers, relevant call/Q&A and estimates. Include material distribution policies, approval/status and payment terms. Establish publication times, units, scope and missing capabilities.
2. Set up versioned executable model state, a calculation backend and stable account/period/unit/dependency maps. Choose the structure from the company's economics using applicable sector guidance. Deliver the required human-auditable Excel forecast alongside the machine interface, preserving user edits when reusing a workbook.
3. Populate and reconcile historical 3S actuals and comparable quarterly/half-year flows. Preserve source/derivation and user edits.
   Complete the [governance review](../../corporate-governance/SKILL.md), beginning collection during intake: control/rights, conduct, incentives, capital allocation and disclosure. Pass material cash-access, dilution and financing consequences into the forecast. Complete the initial decision-driving review before forecasts and deepen it during model audit/valuation; it can reverse a provisional apparent-bargain thesis.
4. Assemble operating/industry data and the driver/segment map. Reconcile conflicts rather than forcing totals.
5. Complete the initial [financial-red-flag screen](../../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md) alongside governance before forecasting. Test reporting reliability, cash/asset quality and financing, preserve benign alternatives and link shared issue IDs. Bound material effects or identify an unusable essential baseline; no clean score is required.
6. Extract dated management guidance and caveats. A lack of disclosed guidance is an explicit input limitation, not a reason to invent it or stop unrelated work.
7. Build a queryable quarterly/half-year revenue/UE module: demand/volumes, realised price/mix, segment revenue and group calibration. A worksheet can expose the module when useful.
8. Build segment costs, gross margins and unit economics from matched bases and supported assumptions.
9. Link those outputs into the forecast income statement, then forecast working capital, capex/depreciation, leases, debt, cash flow and the balance sheet. Include tax, shares, dividends and financing needs. For material distributions, link earnings-year entitlements to payment dates and test recurring cash coverage and payout headroom under the [dividend guide](../../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-driver-and-dividends.md).
10. Execute and audit the completed model, implied remaining periods, cost timing, incentives, shares, cash needs and estimate gaps. Retain the model/engine version, scenario/run, output lineage and check status. Resolve material errors before valuation; workbook backend calculations need real engine verification.
11. Analyse conditional value and price-implied expectations using the audited outputs and governance findings. Distinguish intrinsic value from an attainable target under the current shareholder regime; do not assume an unexplained discount closes. Do not retype outputs into independent valuation assumptions. This is a provisional thesis.
12. Test that thesis against financial/operating evidence, governance discount persistence and the strongest counter-case. Reuse the earlier audit; expand material unresolved issues. Changed forecasts return to model audit and valuation.
13. Synthesise a Buy/Hold/Sell rating, primary target/date, signed price return, bear/bull cases, pivotal insight, catalysts and invalidation. Follow the [decision contract](../../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md): Buy above +10%, Hold within -10% to +10% inclusive, Sell below -10%. Separately assess attractiveness: a Buy below 50% base-case price upside is unattractive under this mandate. Neither the rating nor a 50% upside estimate approves a portfolio allocation. Incomplete research is not a Hold fallback.
14. Produce the brief as the gist of the stock pitch and the fuller visual investment case under the [pitch guide](../../research-brief/references/visual-reports.md). Explain the business, why this price offers the stated payoff, evidence behind forecasts, valuation arithmetic, governance and strongest counter-case. Use charts/tables to support the argument and link technical records separately. Apply the [natural-writing edit](../../research-brief/references/natural-writing.md), recheck fiscal-year/quoted outputs and retain concrete next tasks.

Management questions branch from forecast sensitivities and unresolved validation issues. Ask only what cannot already be established from available sources. An answer returns to the affected assumptions; the source order is not an excuse to wait passively for a nonessential IR response.

## Scope boundary

Portfolio allocation, trading implementation and non-company underwriting require an [external workflow](scope-boundary.md). They are not included company-research stages.

## Existing-research update paths

**Regular review:** read prior research/model and unresolved issues -> check intervening disclosures and concrete data pipelines -> retain operating forecasts unless specific evidence warrants revision -> refresh justified valuation inputs, price and prospective return -> reassess affected thesis/rating/risks -> concise review and next evidence check. A price move changes return to target without automatically changing the target. Supported forecast changes reopen affected model/risk/audit stages; material new economics escalate to the major branch.

**Major earnings/catalyst update:** preserve prior research/model, forecast vintage and user edits -> gather official disclosures, operating data and guidance -> compare matched actuals with prior assumptions and record hits/misses, causes and lessons -> assess changed governance/red flags -> update actuals and recalibrate affected future drivers -> audit model and remaining-period implications -> refresh valuation/expectations -> challenge the revised case -> revise target/call/risks -> refresh affected prose and deliver the revised Excel model when forecasts change. Use an event-impact bridge when a catalyst has no period actuals. Draft questions at material uncertainty; preserve an evidence-supported no-change conclusion.

Use the company workbook profile when importing/editing Excel or delivering a workbook view. The primary result is an executable economic model the agent can query and maintain. Continue analysis within the authorised scope; follow an explicit request for stepwise review when present.

## Recurring-review path

Load the last decision, watch specifications, active-monitor acknowledgements and unresolved tasks. Check data/worker health before interpreting a lack of alerts. Identify whether new information changes facts, thesis, strategy validity, portfolio fit or just price.

A company review's cadence does not determine its research mode. Apply [request routing](request-routing.md): regular review between major events, escalating when material earnings/catalyst evidence requires recalibration.

Examples of selective invalidation:

- Restated actuals -> recheck affected model rows, forecasts, valuation and briefing text.
- New management guidance -> update affected drivers and the scenarios depending on them.
- Changed control, rights, related-party terms or capital allocation -> refresh the governance bridge and affected cash/dilution/value assumptions, target and call.
- Changed price/funding -> refresh instrument economics and portfolio sizing; do not rebuild an unchanged company model.
- Oracle/margin change -> suspend the affected implementation hypothesis and re-evaluate all dependent pairs.
- User changes risk limits -> require the host's mandate revision; recheck every candidate against the approved new version.

Do independent work while a nonessential answer is missing. A critical missing fact blocks the dependent claim or candidate, not unrelated research. A failed data feed is an operational issue with its own next action.

## Priorities and stopping conditions

Rank tasks by their ability to change a decision, material downside, imminence, information gain and cost. A high score is a prioritisation aid, not a fabricated probability. Record what success/failure of a test would mean before running it.

Stop a cycle at its time, step, output-size or compute limit and retain the next useful task. Stop for mandatory input, revoked authority or an uncertain external request where retry could duplicate charges/effects. Do not ask the user to approve another ordinary research read already in scope.

Watch outcomes need an expiry/review date. If a hypothesis repeatedly fails, retire or reformulate it; do not endlessly postpone a decision or lower the evidence bar. Revising the mandate remains the user's choice.
