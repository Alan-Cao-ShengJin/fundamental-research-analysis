# Company research workflow and follow-up

## Scope the work

Start with the research question, exact security, horizon, information cutoff, existing work, available evidence/tools and requested deliverable. [Request routing](request-routing.md) selects initiation, regular review or major earnings/catalyst update. [Company sequence](company-sequence.md) defines construction, review and publication dependencies. Reuse eligible work and preserve prior evidence and forecast vintages.

Portfolio construction, position sizing, cross-asset strategy and account monitoring are outside this research module. Company research can supply dated forecasts, risks and conclusions to that separate work; it does not assign portfolio percentages or authorize implementation.

## Focused research

Use the relevant [research branch](research-branches.md) for evidence intake, screening, macro/industry questions, financial review, opinion challenge or company changes. A scoped statement question does not require an initiation. A completed stock call still needs the company construction and validation stages. Issuer questions branch from decision-sensitive gaps; drafting them does not authorize contact.

## Company follow-up

Use [company change tracking](../../company-change-tracking/SKILL.md) and the [company follow-up guide](company-follow-up.md) to retain questions, expected observations, due dates and frozen-forecast learning. Authorized recurring research uses self-contained tasks with explicit baselines, cadence/timezone, missing-data rules and completion conditions. A saved plan does not establish a running scheduler.

At review, check data freshness and health before interpreting silence. Apply the existing regular/major routing; cadence alone does not determine the mode. Restated actuals reopen affected calculations, new guidance reopens supported driver assumptions, governance changes reopen shareholder-access findings, and price-only changes refresh valuation/return without automatically rebuilding the operating forecast. Preserve unresolved questions and valid no-change findings.

## Priorities and stopping

Rank the next read or calculation by decision materiality, urgency, information value and cost. Stop at the scoped deliverable or resource boundary and retain the next useful task. A missing essential input blocks dependent claims, while unrelated research can continue. Keep proposed recurrence, actual scheduling and delivery acknowledgements separate. Research completion does not authorize portfolio action, outreach or trading.
