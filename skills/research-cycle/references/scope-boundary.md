# Scope boundary

This standalone bundle covers company fundamental research, human-auditable financial models, governance, valuation, research conclusions and proposed follow-up. It does not contain portfolio allocation, risk budgeting, strategy backtesting, trade execution or non-company underwriting skills.

References to those external workflows describe a handoff boundary, not installed tools or promised capabilities. A stock rating, target, conviction record or planned watch does not choose a weight, place an order, send a message or activate monitoring. Keep company economic research separate from exact instrument mechanics and account-specific authorization.

When asked for an external task, state the missing workflow/capability and retain the useful research handoff. Do not fabricate company financial statements for an index, commodity, currency or token. Actual monitoring and communication need the host's available services and the user's authorization.
