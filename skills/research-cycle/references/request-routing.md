# Govern the research request

Choose the research mode before selecting specialist work. This routing governs company research; sector guidance supplies relevant economic mechanisms within a mode, rather than creating another workflow. Record the mode, security, information cutoff, baseline model/research versions, trigger and requested outputs in the existing research brief.

Updates retrieve the original [conviction record](../../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) before interpreting new commentary, and separate a change in the decisive belief from changes in forecast, valuation, rating, eligibility and portfolio effects.

## Two branches, three working modes

Follow the selected route and reuse valid upstream work. Reopen specialists where new evidence could change the call; do not force every specialist into a regular review.

| Branch | Working mode | Trigger | Work and stopping point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New initiation | Initiation | The user asks for an initiation, or no usable coverage exists | Establish the business, historical and operating baseline; build forecasts with relevant sector guidance; complete risk review, model audit, valuation, challenge and initial investment view. Freeze the baseline and deliver the human-auditable Excel model. |
| Existing research update | Regular review | Revisit an existing case between major disclosures, often because time or price has moved | Read the previous case and model; check intervening evidence; retain operating assumptions by default. Refresh justified valuation inputs, current price, prospective return and conclusion. Link the unchanged model when no forecast revision is warranted. |
| Existing research update | Major earnings/catalyst update | Results or a material event provides new evidence requiring substantial reassessment | Preserve the pre-event forecast; reconcile official disclosures and calls; evaluate prior assumptions; replace relevant estimates with actuals and recalibrate subsequent forecasts. Audit affected dependencies and update valuation, thesis and conclusion. |

Use [stock initiation](../../stock-initiation/SKILL.md) for the first branch and [model revision](../../model-revision/SKILL.md) for both update modes. In the catalog, `company-regular-review` is the regular flow and `company-update` the major-update flow; the revision entry requires mode selection.

## Regular review: preserve the forecast, test the evidence

1. Load the latest valid research, assumptions, model, valuation, unresolved issues and next earnings/catalysts. Identify stale or defective dependencies; a previous approval does not make an error reusable.
2. Rerun the [world-state scan](world-state.md) for the period since the last review, re-establish [what the market is trading](what-the-market-trades.md) (the live variable and period can have moved on), and check relevant new disclosures and defined data pipelines. Concrete evidence can include comparable operating statistics, dated management guidance, credible third-party demand/price/cost trackers or verified policy changes. Retain source, period, definitions, coverage, lag and the economic link to the affected assumption. A rumor, price move, peer rerating or general narrative alone does not justify a new operating forecast. An unavailable feed does not prove assumptions remain correct.
3. Carry forward operating forecasts unless that evidence supports a specific revision. Small supported changes can remain a regular review with targeted recalculation/audit and a visible old/new bridge. Escalate to the major branch if evidence materially changes the economics, reporting perimeter or thesis; frequency or the title of a document does not decide the route.
4. Refresh the quote, prospective price return and dividend yield on the retained earnings/cash forecasts. Roll the target date to twelve months from the review date and re-derive the target from that day's forward earnings. Distinguish price movement from a changed target: price alone changes upside/downside, not EPS or the underlying value. Explain any change to FX, valuation multiple, discount rate or earnings reference year separately. Apply the existing fiscal-calendar rules; elapsed time or a higher share price is not sufficient reason to increase a multiple or roll earnings silently.
5. Report what changed, what was checked, whether operating forecasts are unchanged and why, current valuation/decision, and the next decisive data. A regular review may change the rating because price changed while retaining the same target and forecast. Do not regenerate the full initiation or workbook solely to create a new dated artifact.

## Major update: evaluate, then recalibrate

1. Preserve the prior forecast vintage, information cutoff and calculation outputs before replacing estimates. Preserve later pre-event revisions separately so their information sets are not confused with initiation.
2. Gather official results/event disclosures, statement and segment detail, operating breakdowns, management guidance and Q&A and the covering brokers' notes on the event, rerun the [world-state scan](world-state.md) for the period since the last review and re-establish [what the market is trading](what-the-market-trades.md): results usually move the live question. Retain company-reported facts separately from management explanations and analyst estimates. Identify missing disclosures; do not fill them with broker forecasts presented as actuals.
3. Compare matched prior estimates and assumptions with new actuals before rolling periods. Show material metric/period, prior estimate, actual, absolute/percentage difference where meaningful, and driver explanation. Reconcile accounting scope and restatements. Mark assumptions as supported, missed, mixed or not yet observable; offsetting errors can make a correct total misleading. New guidance about a future period informs recalibration but cannot score that future forecast as correct or wrong yet.
4. Record what was learned: which foundational assumptions worked, which failed, what the original evidence supported, and whether the cause was an economic change, timing, bad proxy, data/definition error, model defect or unresolved factor. Distinguish information unavailable at the time from a preventable miss. Keep issuer facts and numerical calibration in the research record; promote a lesson to generic skills only when it generalizes beyond one outcome.
5. Reassess the governance and financial-quality issues the new evidence raises, then update historical/model actuals and revise affected foundational drivers, revenue, costs, working capital, investment and funding. Use applicable sector guidance when it helps diagnose a mechanism. Reconcile remaining-quarter/half implications; do not mechanically extrapolate one period or reset every segment. A catalyst without period results receives an event-impact bridge and scenario update, not invented earnings actuals.
6. Audit the revised model, then update valuation, run the fresh model/evidence challenge ([company sequence](company-sequence.md), stage 14) and update the investment conclusion. Attribute operating, market-price, FX, calendar and valuation-method changes separately. Deliver an updated formula-driven Excel model when forecasts change, plus a concise revision and learning record linked to the old vintage.

An earnings release requires this evaluation even when it confirms the case; unchanged future assumptions can be the result. A catalyst review can remain partial if essential event terms are unavailable. Broader reconstruction is justified by invalid foundations, not merely by the arrival of another quarter. Preserve uncertainties and next checks in either mode.

## Specialist use follows the mode

Initiation uses the full [company sequence](company-sequence.md), including sector-guided modeling. Regular reviews emphasize evidence screening, valuation and decision refresh; invoke modeling, risk and challenge work only where affected, reusing valid upstream artifacts. Major updates emphasize evidence reconciliation, forecast-to-actual learning and model recalibration before downstream decisions. Portfolio sizing, implementation and activated monitoring remain separate authorized tasks.
