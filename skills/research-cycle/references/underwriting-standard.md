# Evidence and underwriting standard

Use for company initiation and material updates; apply only relevant tests to focused questions. Arithmetic integrity supports research but does not establish economic truth. Apply [investor judgment](investor-judgment.md) under imperfect information: educated estimates are normal; seek a defensible central case rather than certainty.

## Integrate the analysis by materiality

Conversation order, document position and the date a technique was added do not rank importance. Connect business economics/competition, financial quality and funded growth, governance/shareholder access, valuation/expectations, market context and probability/payoff. Choose depth by this security's economics, price, horizon and decision sensitivity. No dimension has fixed weight or dominates every case. The sequence expresses dependencies; reuse evidence and count each economic effect once.

## Decision-driving assumptions

For each assumption that can change the call, show its value, basis, source and the range that would change the call beside the calculation. Preserve underlying observations separately from attributed guidance and analyst judgment. Missing values stay null, not zero. A source supporting a trend does not substantiate a particular numerical elasticity.

Align current evidence and comparable periods on the [earnings clock](../../company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md); preserve source availability before inferring surprises, peer signals or what was priced in.

A plausible adverse case reversing the call is normal. Explain why the central case deserves more weight, margin for error, financing survival and evidence that would change the view. Give a call on completed, supportable research under the [decision contract](../../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md).

## Economics, funding and shareholder access

Follow [competition and capital returns](../../company-model/references/competition-and-capital-returns.md): customer choice, competitor/supplier responses, pricing, reinvestment, marginal returns and advantage duration. Growth creates value only when its funded returns justify the capital. Use the economic unit appropriate to the business and reconcile its aggregation to reported figures.

Distinguish sell-in from sell-through, surviving cohorts from the full population, organic from acquired growth, bookings from recognised revenue and collected cash, capacity from saleable output, and consolidated cash from cash available to common shareholders. For operating proxies, check coverage, methodology, selection/survivor bias, lag and historical calibration; unvalidated proxies are directional context or scenario inputs.

Apply [financial red-flag analysis](../../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md) and governance during initiation or when evidence raises new issues. Test benign explanations, separate confidence from severity, and carry material cash-access, dilution and funding effects into forecasts without double counting. An aggregate score or cheap valuation cannot offset a critical threat.

Explain material cash recoveries through working capital, tax and seasonality. Reconcile investment, debt, leases, compensation, dividends and buybacks to sustainable cash and shareholder claims using [statement forecasting](../../company-model/references/statement-forecast.md). Missing regulatory-capital data cannot prove distribution capacity. Compare material management promises with subsequent delivery in the relevant assumption notes rather than a second ledger.

## Valuation and return

Integrate [market context](../../macro-event-analysis/references/market-regime-and-investor-base.md) where material, keeping economic exposure distinct from narrative association. Neither named future buyers nor proven inflows are required. Explain [probability versus payoff](investor-judgment.md#separate-probability-of-success-from-the-payoff-at-the-price): likely business success can still offer a poor return at the price.

Use an executable bridge separating earnings/operating change, multiple change, cash/debt/share effects and distributions. State interaction conventions and show the outcome if today's valuation persists. Match claims, earnings horizons, accounting and estimate vintages across methods. Explain material method disagreements rather than selecting the result that suits the thesis.

A dated target needs a credible route to realisation within the horizon: earnings and cash delivery, distributions or a supported re-rating. A known dated catalyst is usually already priced in. A low multiple, a known weakness or an undated hope of reform alone does not establish mispricing. Apply the [valuation sequence](../../valuation-thesis/SKILL.md#value-the-company-in-six-steps).

## Challenge and subsequent learning

Apply [empirical validation](empirical-research-and-validation.md): reproduce pivotal arithmetic and verify important evidence. Test a coherent adverse case combining demand, margins, working capital, funding, dilution and valuation. Distinguish volatility from permanent loss, insolvency or inaccessible value; a bear target is not a loss floor.

Challenge influential assumptions against contrary primary evidence. Freeze the forecast vintage before actuals arrive; match period, perimeter and accounting, then separate operational error, data changes, allocation and valuation error. Use old/new/actual/reason notes for changed assumptions. Report only checks performed and services actually running; the skills do not install a runtime.
