# Optional writing examples and terminology

The short [writing standard](natural-writing.md) governs drafting and review. Open this reference only for a specific terminology question; it is not a second checklist to load in full.

This is serious research that people invest on: write nothing you cannot support. Every fact traces to a filing, data series, attributed comment or the model; every interpretation is ours and has been tested against the data that could contradict it. If we do not know something, say so or leave it out; never fill a gap with a plausible-sounding sentence, an unsourced comparison or a generic industry belief. Do not place unrelated facts side by side so they read as cause and effect. Before release, reread every sentence of the report and brief against the evidence and delete or qualify what does not hold.

## Profit labels and valuation language

Use **net profit attributable to shareholders** for the listed company's earnings after excluding non-controlling interests. After defining it, **attributable net profit** is a concise label. Do not call it parent profit or substitute group profit, operating profit, adjusted earnings or cash flow. Internal identifiers can remain stable when their displayed definitions are correct.

Introduce a valuation assertion with its period and earnings basis: "The shares trade at approximately 25x forecast FY2028 core EPS." Explain the calculation nearby and justify the multiple. Preserve reasonable headline rounding, with exact supporting calculations available. Apply the [target and earnings-basis contract](../../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md#target-and-earnings-basis).

For a multiple-based target, keep the dated benchmark, earnings window, reason for the selected level and observable repricing mechanism in the argument. Distinguish a provider-reported mean from a calculated history, and a market observation from fair value. A target-price multiplication or a statement that the multiple changes does not supply the investment reasoning.

Use **multiple expansion** or **multiple contraction** when that is the return driver. Explain the operating evidence, allocation change or risk reassessment that supports it. A reference to sentiment or investor confidence alone does not justify a selected multiple.

Distinguish adjusted earnings from cash after investment, price return from dividend yield, economic value from a realisable target, and business quality from the return on offer at the price. Apply the [decision contract](../../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md).
