# Stock pitch, decision brief and research update

Read the current decision and underlying versioned artifacts. Write in English unless requested otherwise, for an informed investor who understands business models but not company-specific jargon. Use chat unless a file or other deliverable is requested; preserve an explicit template, audience or length.

For a company pitch or update, use the current [conviction record](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) to explain the decisive reason to remain invested or avoid the company, what evidence now says, what would change the view and the next decisive observation. Keep a binding exclusion visible beside the call. Distinguish retained business belief from a changed forecast, target, price-based rating or investment eligibility; do not rewrite the original thesis around the latest price. A brief paragraph is sufficient, without a conviction score or an extra report chapter.

## Select what matters

Apply the [shared underwriting standard](../research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) and [investor judgment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md). Choose emphasis by decision materiality, not conversation order. Integrate business economics/moat, funded growth, financial quality, governance, valuation/expectations, market context and probability/payoff as relevant. No dimension deserves a default chapter, fixed weight or leading position merely because it was discussed recently.

Explain the core money-making engine, why customers choose the business and the pivotal reason for the call. Identify the supported difference from priced or attributed expectations and how it affects shareholder return. Define issuer-specific jargon. If no overlooked driver is established, explain the valuation/return judgment without inventing a market mistake.

Write the argument directly: what happens, why, and what it means for the share price. Apply the [writing and terminology guide](references/natural-writing.md#profit-labels-and-valuation-language): use net profit attributable to shareholders, introduce valuation claims before their calculations, and explain why investors would pay a higher P/E. Cut indirect phrases that obscure the point.

A full company recommendation is a stock pitch: make a defensible investment argument and prove it. Lead with why this price offers, or fails to offer, an attractive payoff. Organise evidence around that argument rather than narrating the research sequence or listing account changes. Persuasion requires a clear causal explanation, valuation and strongest counter-case; it does not justify cherry-picking or preserving a call after its numbers change.

Include competitive response, marginal returns, advantage duration, narrative association or investor-access detail when it changes the decision. Distinguish current economic exposure from a possible market association. Investor-demand analysis normally remains supporting detail; material liquidity, funding or rights concerns remain visible. Explain estimates, decisive evidence and revision conditions without generic commentary about source motives.

## Decision and risk block

Follow the [stock decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md) for completed stock recommendations: one Buy/Hold/Sell, exact security and current quote/time, one primary target/date, signed price return and same-horizon bear/bull targets/returns. Put one key thesis sentence immediately beside/below the call. Confidence follows the label; Trim/Avoid/Watch cannot replace it.

Separate rating from attractiveness under the decision contract. Above +10% primary-target price return is Buy, -10% to +10% inclusive is Hold, and below -10% is Sell. A Buy with less than 50% base-case price upside is unattractive under this mandate; say so directly without changing the rating. At least 50% upside still needs a credible target and acceptable risk. Retain target, scenarios and thesis; do not inflate them to qualify. Incomplete research is a scope/status, not a Hold fallback. A Sell is not automatically a short recommendation.

Distinguish primary target, today's intrinsic value, expected return, dividend contribution and confidence. Explain the primary valuation method and material disagreement with alternatives. Show whether return depends on operating delivery, distributions or multiple/narrative change. A high probability of business success does not establish an attractive payoff. Use qualitative likelihoods when numerical weights lack support; a bear case losing money does not make the central call incoherent.

Name the [valuation driver](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-driver-and-dividends.md). If the pitch rests on dividends, state annual DPS/yield, payout ratio, cash coverage and room for a higher payout, then explain why investors would accept the target yield. If it rests on P/E rerating, state the specific change and evidence that justify it. Do not use dividends as a vague reason for an unexplained higher P/E. Keep annual yield separate from distributions receivable within the target horizon.

## Pitch structure and depth

Match the output to the workflow. [Stock initiation](../stock-initiation/SKILL.md) explains the complete investment case. [Model revision](../model-revision/SKILL.md) normally produces a short change note: evidence and reasons, old/new assumptions and earnings/cash/target, whether the call or conviction changes, and remaining uncertainty. Separate price/FX/calendar effects from operating revisions. A no-material-change result needs its evidence, not a forced forecast. Refresh affected existing figures and decision blocks without rewriting unchanged business background. Reissue a full pitch when requested or when the old thesis is misleading.

Use the [earnings clock](../company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md) internally to understand the current information set. Fresh evidence is expected; do not add a routine latest-data declaration, report-age line or earnings-clock block to every investment brief. Include a future event/date and its confirmed/estimated status when it materially supports the thesis or target horizon. Explain decisive recent/peer evidence in context, while judging the whole business, longer-term economics, governance, valuation and payoff rather than one quarter. A specifically requested earnings note can focus more narrowly.

Translate source evidence into the user's preferred output language, English by default. For Chinese AlphaPai material, preserve the original privately with its locator and accurately translate speaker roles, reporting periods, qualification and units. A translated management statement remains an attributed claim; do not silently strengthen its certainty or present a generated/ASR note as a verified original quotation.

Read [visual pitch design](references/visual-reports.md) and apply the [natural-writing edit](references/natural-writing.md), adapted from Humanizer. The decision brief is the gist of the pitch, normally one page. The full visual pitch develops the argument across material business, competitive, operating, financial, governance, valuation and risk angles; it is substantially more detailed than the brief. Do not compress full research into a second summary or send the reader to an appendix to discover the argument. Detailed statements, source logs and implementation records remain supporting material.

Introduce each material number with its meaning and explain its investment consequence. A valuation mentioned in the opening must be defined and reproduced nearby from its earnings period, profit/EPS basis, shares, currency and price. Use natural headline precision, such as about 5x earnings, with exact calculations in the valuation panel. Adjusted profit is not cash after investment. Apply the [valuation calendar](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-calendar.md): the price-target date does not silently advance the chosen forward earnings year.

Use [chart visualization](../chart-visualization/SKILL.md) for AntV construction and the shared professional theme. Each figure should replace explanation, use current model/evidence data, distinguish actual/forecast values and preserve essential information without hover or JavaScript. Inspect the rendered desktop/mobile/export result as applicable; text inspection alone does not verify layout. Avoid decorative charts and tables filled with paragraph-length text.

## Evidence and final verification

Recompute quoted values from current artifacts. Check period/YTD, reported/adjusted basis, currency/scale, basic/diluted shares, quote status/date and same-horizon scenario returns. Preserve management qualifiers. Separate prospective dividends from price upside, avoid counting past distributions or buybacks twice, and keep rounding consistent. Use the applicable rendering workflow for requested document/spreadsheet formats.

Attribute material claims with readable links/footnotes near the claim or a compact source list; retain precise locators and internal IDs in support. Do not erase provenance or invent proprietary coverage. For updates, distinguish new evidence from changed assumptions and decompose material forecast/valuation revisions. For portfolio updates, separate market movement, changed thesis, position changes, costs and data-health failures.

Keep the strongest counter-case, material governance/shareholder-access consequences, decisive uncertainties and the next event/evidence that would change the view. Include monitoring status when requested; silence does not prove an active healthy service. Describe only validation actually performed.

For material [red flags](../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md), state the observation, confidence and consequence for minority shareholders beside the relevant thesis/risk. Severe governance concerns deserve high-priority visibility; reporting/reliability concerns can also decide the case. Use precise attributed language rather than presenting suspicion as fraud. Keep the account-level test log linked separately, preserve compact reading budgets and avoid adding a compulsory scorecard or audit report.

## Scope boundaries

Exclude internal check counts, run hashes, commands, agent activity and billing detail from investor reports. Do not generate, save or report token/model costs unless the current request asks; economically relevant company/trading/funding costs remain part of analysis.

Preserve user-authored final wording as requested and flag material factual disagreements separately. Label saved drafts/final copies accurately. Reporting does not authorize publishing, outreach, trading or monitor activation; follow the host's existing authorization for external actions.
