# Stock pitch, decision brief and research update

Read the current decision and underlying versioned artifacts. Write in English unless requested otherwise, for an informed investor who understands business models but not company-specific jargon. Use chat unless a file or other deliverable is requested; preserve an explicit template, audience or length.

For an **investment brief / one-pager**, follow [the one-page guide](references/one-page-brief.md): exactly one readable A4 page using the shared template. Select the sections and headings that best explain this decision. Keep the call, its decisive rationale, reproducible valuation and strongest counter-case; add background, financial metrics, catalysts or governance detail only when material. Use the blank payload and render/check scripts linked there. Apply the institutional editorial standard, verify the actual PDF has one page and visually inspect it. The full report and model remain linked support.

Use [investment conviction](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) to preserve the decisive proposition and active exclusions across reviews.

## Select what matters

For house stock briefs, the narrative develops business drivers into earnings/cash, our forecast versus comparable expectations, valuation and the strongest risk. The header can state the call and target immediately; it does not replace that causal reading order. Use the [synthetic calibration example](references/calibration-example.md) to assess the reasoning, not to copy its wording or business assumptions.

Apply a deletion test to every paragraph, metric and visual: would removing it change the assessment of earnings, cash generation, value, risk or what would change the view? If not, omit it. Combine overlapping points; do not repeat the headline and target throughout the page. A skill's analytical checklist governs the research performed, not a quota of material to publish. Routine governance summaries, familiar company descriptions, uncertain calendar dates and unused statistics do not earn space merely because they were checked.

Do not confuse selection with suppressing contrary evidence. Retain a binding exclusion, a material alternative valuation disagreement, a forecast-critical qualification, the core debate the stock turns on and the strongest counter-case. Put detailed evidence, definitions and routine checks in linked support.

Apply the [shared underwriting standard](../research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) and [investor judgment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md). Publish the findings that matter to this investment argument, rather than a section for each analytical topic.

Open with a compact statement of [what the market is trading](../research-cycle/references/what-the-market-trades.md): the variable and period that set the price, what the price implies for it and where our reading differs. Detailed bands, peer tables and window alternatives follow the business and its drivers in the valuation section. Give each business space in proportion to its share of value, not its share of reported revenue; a legacy franchise the price has moved past is context.

Explain the principal reason for the call and the economic mechanism behind it. In an initiation, establish how each material business earns profit, who buys from it and what protects it before developing the detailed forecast and valuation; in an update, repeat business context only where needed to follow a change. Identify the supported difference from priced or attributed expectations and how it affects shareholder return. Define issuer-specific jargon. If no overlooked driver is established, explain the valuation/return judgment without inventing a market mistake.

Build each substantive investment point in this sequence: **state the point and data; explain how the data was derived; explain its significance; tie the implication back to the view on the stock.** Follow the [argument sequence](references/natural-writing.md#argument-sequence) in concise, connected prose. These are reasoning steps within a paragraph or related paragraphs, not four printed headings or a quota of sentences. The reader should see the causal chain without opening the model. Apply the [writing and terminology guide](references/natural-writing.md#profit-labels-and-valuation-language): use net profit attributable to shareholders, introduce valuation claims before their calculations, and explain why investors would pay a higher P/E. Cut indirect phrases that obscure the point.

A full company recommendation is a stock pitch: make a defensible investment argument and prove it. Lead with why this price offers, or fails to offer, an attractive payoff. Organise evidence around that argument rather than narrating the research sequence or listing account changes. Persuasion requires a clear causal explanation, valuation and strongest counter-case; it does not justify cherry-picking or preserving a call after its numbers change.

Include competitive response, marginal returns, advantage duration, narrative association or investor-access detail when it changes the decision. Distinguish current economic exposure from a possible market association. Investor-demand analysis normally remains supporting detail; material liquidity, funding or rights concerns remain visible. Explain estimates, decisive evidence and revision conditions without generic commentary about source motives.

Complete essential company, forecast and valuation work before writing the brief. Use the four [target-underwriting checks](../valuation-thesis/references/target-underwriting.md) to support a qualified central judgment, rather than publishing a process memo about missing work.

For a full report, begin writing in a fresh context from the frozen analysis checkpoint. Load the short [writing standard](references/natural-writing.md), the selected output template and the relevant financial definitions, retaining access to original pivotal evidence. Develop each conclusion in one main location; a summary may repeat the takeaway, while later sections refer back briefly. The separate fresh review and finalization follow the [publication workflow](../report-layout/references/publication-review.md).

## Decision and risk block

Follow the [stock decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md) for completed stock recommendations: one Buy/Hold/Sell, exact security and current quote/time, one primary target/date, signed price return, how the target differs from consensus and why, and same-horizon bear/bull targets/returns with little emphasis. Put one key thesis sentence immediately beside/below the call. Confidence follows the label; Trim/Avoid/Watch cannot replace it.

Apply the [decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md) for rating, price return beside the call, and dividend yield shown separately. The new-money tier and entry prices stay in the decision record, not the brief.

Explain the primary valuation method; the dated anchor supporting its multiple or pivotal input; earnings versus valuation contributions on explicit endpoints (zero earnings-period roll when current-forward EPS is unchanged); and the outcome if today’s multiple persists. Use the [target and earnings-basis contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md#target-and-earnings-basis).

Name the [valuation driver](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-driver-and-dividends.md). If the pitch rests on dividends, state annual DPS/yield, payout ratio, cash coverage and room for a higher payout, then explain why investors would accept the target yield. If it rests on P/E rerating, state the specific change and evidence that justify it. Do not use dividends as a vague reason for an unexplained higher P/E. Keep annual yield separate from distributions receivable within the target horizon.

## Pitch structure and depth

Match the output to the workflow. [Stock initiation](../stock-initiation/SKILL.md) explains the complete investment case. [Model revision](../model-revision/SKILL.md) normally produces a short change note: evidence and reasons, old/new assumptions and earnings/cash/target, whether the call or conviction changes, and remaining uncertainty. Separate price/FX/calendar effects from operating revisions. A no-material-change result needs its evidence, not a forced forecast. Refresh affected existing figures and decision blocks without rewriting unchanged business background. Reissue a full pitch when requested or when the old thesis is misleading.

Use the [earnings clock](../company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md) internally to understand the current information set. Fresh evidence is expected; do not add a routine latest-data declaration, report-age line or earnings-clock block to every investment brief. Include a future event/date and its confirmed/estimated status when it materially supports the thesis or target horizon. Explain decisive recent/peer evidence in context, while judging the whole business, longer-term economics, governance, valuation and payoff rather than one quarter. A specifically requested earnings note can focus more narrowly.

Translate source evidence into the requested output language, English by default. For non-English source material, preserve the original privately with its locator and accurately translate speaker roles, reporting periods, qualification and units. A translated management statement remains an attributed claim; do not silently strengthen its certainty or present a generated/ASR note as a verified original quotation.

Read [visual pitch design](references/visual-reports.md) and apply the [institutional research writing standard](references/natural-writing.md). After figures are settled, edit for a formal analyst voice, precise financial terminology, concision and attribution; then verify that financial claims, uncertainty and sources survived. The brief follows the selective one-page guide above. The full visual pitch develops the argument across material business, industry, competitive, operating, financial, governance, valuation and risk angles; it is substantially more detailed. Its industry section comes before the forecasts and explains how industry growth, the company's niche, barrier trends, upstream costs and customer behaviour shape the assumptions; it is not a description of the industry for its own sake. Deliver it as an A4 PDF in the house [report layout](../report-layout/SKILL.md) and inspect the pages; the HTML is the source, not the deliverable. Do not compress full research into a second summary or send the reader to an appendix to discover the argument. Detailed statements, source logs and implementation records remain supporting material.

For a material number, make the source or forecast build visible before interpreting it. A metric definition, growth comparison or statement that a figure comes from our model does not explain its derivation. Identify the evidence, assumptions and calculation that produce the result; use a relevant comparison to explain its significance, then connect that significance to earnings, value, risk or conviction in the stock. Explain unfamiliar terms only as needed for that reasoning. Omit a metric table if it adds no inference beyond the surrounding prose. A valuation mentioned in the opening must be defined and reproduced nearby from its earnings period, profit/EPS basis, shares, currency and price. Use natural headline precision, such as about 5x earnings, with exact calculations in the valuation panel. Adjusted profit is not cash after investment. Apply the [valuation calendar](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-calendar.md): the target applies the target multiple to today's forward earnings, and the brief says which period that is. Present the multiple as a reasoned target, not as fair value, and explain the economic anchor without narrating the research process.

Use the shared report renderer and [chart visualization](../chart-visualization/SKILL.md) theme; AntV is optional for visuals the built-in renderer does not supply. Each figure should replace explanation, use current model/evidence data, distinguish actual/forecast values and preserve essential information without hover or JavaScript. Inspect the rendered desktop/mobile/export result as applicable; text inspection alone does not verify layout. Avoid decorative charts and tables filled with paragraph-length text.

## Evidence and final verification

Write from the synchronized [decision checkpoint](../report-layout/references/decision-record.md) and bind material prose calculations/ranges using the [shared display contract](../report-layout/references/claim-support.md#feasible-bridges-and-actual-displayed-figures). Keep a negative cash-flow sign distinct from a positive payout magnitude, and preserve the relevant payment/earnings year and constraint deadline. A conditional bridge to aggregate earnings must not become a claim about the only possible contributor assumptions when shortened for the brief.

Recompute quoted values from current artifacts. Check period/YTD, reported/adjusted basis, currency/scale, basic/diluted shares, quote status/date and same-horizon scenario returns. Preserve management qualifiers. Separate prospective dividends from price upside, avoid counting past distributions or buybacks twice, and keep rounding consistent. Use the applicable rendering workflow for requested document/spreadsheet formats.

During revisions, preserve sound and accepted arguments outside the affected scope. Before publishing, the drafted report and brief go through the [research challenge](../research-challenge/SKILL.md) second pass, and check that the new detail has not crowded out the drivers, derivation, investment significance or counter-case. A correctly formatted page with accurate arithmetic can still fail as an investment argument. For workflow improvement rather than another report, follow [skill calibration](../research-cycle/references/skill-calibration.md).

Attribute material claims with readable links/footnotes near the claim or a compact source list in the full report (the one-page brief normally carries none); retain precise locators and internal IDs in support. Do not erase provenance or invent proprietary coverage. For updates, distinguish new evidence from changed assumptions and decompose material forecast/valuation revisions. For portfolio updates, separate market movement, changed thesis, position changes, costs and data-health failures.

Keep the strongest counter-case, material governance/shareholder-access consequences, decisive uncertainties and the next event/evidence that would change the view. Include monitoring status when requested; silence does not prove an active healthy service. Describe only validation actually performed.

For material [red flags](../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md), state the observation, confidence and consequence for minority shareholders beside the relevant thesis/risk. Severe governance concerns deserve high-priority visibility; reporting/reliability concerns can also decide the case. Use precise attributed language rather than presenting suspicion as fraud. Keep the account-level test log linked separately, preserve compact reading budgets and avoid adding a compulsory scorecard or audit report.

## Scope boundaries

Exclude internal check counts, run hashes, commands, agent activity and billing detail from investor reports. Do not generate, save or report token/model costs unless the current request asks; economically relevant company/trading/funding costs remain part of analysis.

Preserve user-authored final wording as requested and flag material factual disagreements separately. Label saved drafts/final copies accurately. Reporting does not authorize publishing, outreach, trading or monitor activation; follow the host's existing authorization for external actions.
