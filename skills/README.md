# Investment research skills

Use [research-cycle](research-cycle/SKILL.md) for end-to-end work, or select a specialist for a focused request. Instructions are in English and apply across issuers and markets. Adapt the analysis and output language to an explicit user request.

Investment briefs follow the [one-page house format](research-brief/references/one-page-brief.md): selective decision-useful content, flexible headings and the shared issuer-neutral minimalist template, an institutional editorial review and one verified A4 page. Each substantive point follows [data, derivation, significance and stock-view implication](research-brief/references/natural-writing.md#argument-sequence) in connected prose. The full investment report remains separate. Reusable payload/render/check assets live with research-brief; formatting changes preserve the current model and decision.

The governing [request routing](research-cycle/references/request-routing.md) distinguishes **new initiation** from **existing research updates**. Initiation establishes the full baseline and initial view with relevant sector guidance. Existing updates use [model revision](model-revision/SKILL.md) in **regular-review mode**, retaining operating forecasts unless concrete evidence supports a change, or **major earnings/catalyst mode**, evaluating prior assumptions against disclosures before recalibrating forecasts. The two workflow entry skills are `stock-initiation` and `model-revision`. The catalog flows `company-regular-review` and `company-update` describe the regular and major branches of model-revision, alongside eighteen analytical and presentation specialists. Both retain shared modeling, risk and decision standards; cadence alone does not select a mode.

## Research principles and sequence

Use [investment conviction](investment-conviction/SKILL.md) across research vintages: preserve one primary economic or avoidance proposition, supporting/contrary evidence, original milestones and conditions for reconsideration. An intact belief can survive price volatility while valuation and attractiveness change. A supported ongoing value-diversion condition can keep a company excluded despite cheapness. The default investment horizon is at least a year unless the mandate specifies another; it does not require retaining a broken thesis. Completed records belong in company research, using the clean blank scaffold; no live track record or automatic position action is implied.

Use the [underwriting standard](research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) and [investor judgment](research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md) as the shared analytical foundation. Integrate business economics/competition, financial quality and funded growth, governance/shareholder access, valuation/expectations, macro/market context and probability/payoff. Choose emphasis by the security, price, horizon and decision sensitivity; conversation order and document position do not determine importance. No dimension has a fixed weight or automatic precedence.

Reasoned estimates and differentiated opinions are normal. Make a defensible central return/risk judgment without requiring perfect information or success in every scenario. Separate probability of success from its payoff at today's price. Retain source-specific credibility, uncertainty, adverse cases and observable revision conditions.

Use [industry analysis](industry-analysis/SKILL.md) before operating forecasts so that growth, share, price, cost and margin assumptions rest on the industry (its growth, the company's niche, barrier trends, upstream costs and downstream behaviour), and its indicators in every review. Use [competition and capital-return analysis](company-model/references/competition-and-capital-returns.md), [market context and narratives](macro-event-analysis/references/market-regime-and-investor-base.md) and [empirical/source validation](research-cycle/references/empirical-research-and-validation.md) within the existing stages as material. Topical association can affect market price before earnings emerge without creating reported revenue. Investor-demand detail remains supporting analysis unless decisive. Instructions, empirical records and actual operating services are distinct.

Investor output is a supported **stock pitch**: a one-page decision brief gives its gist, while the fuller visual pitch develops the business, evidence, forecasts, governance, valuation, return path and counter-case. Explain headline valuation arithmetic in the pitch and state the earnings period behind the target. Technical models/evidence remain linked support. Apply [visual report design](research-brief/references/visual-reports.md), the [institutional research writing guide](research-brief/references/natural-writing.md) and [AntV chart visualization](chart-visualization/SKILL.md). Figures share a professional theme and preserve source/actual/forecast distinctions.

The sequence below expresses dependencies rather than a ranking of importance. Context and evidence can develop together, and material new findings reopen affected stages.

**Scope/mode -> evidence -> model setup -> historical statements -> corporate governance and early financial-red-flag screening -> industry analysis -> operating drivers and guidance -> revenue and cost projections -> integrated statement forecasts -> model audit -> valuation and expectations -> thesis validation and challenge -> conviction assessment -> Buy/Hold/Sell and eligibility -> brief and follow-up.**

Before concluding an initiation, confirm governance, financial-quality and valuation work was done; use other specialists when they could change the call.

Governance and [financial-red-flag collection](financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md) start during intake; at initiation, the initial reviews precede forecasts. Evidence-supported threats to minority value, whether governance abuse or unreliable accounts, receive high-priority triage; either can decide the case. One financial-quality skill performs early screening, model audit and post-valuation validation with shared issue records. A focused red-flag review can use filings directly. Governance findings must reach the model and valuation before the conclusion. Cheapness can reflect a justified, persistent discount, so identify how value reaches outside shareholders and what could change the discount. Full company updates refresh the existing governance record when material evidence changes.

Completed recommendations follow the [decision contract](investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md), including price return beside the rating, dividend yield shown separately and a separate new-money judgment.

The company model is executable, versioned economic state for agent queries, scenarios, valuation and forecast evaluation. Company forecast builds and numerical revisions also require the [human-auditable formula-driven Excel model](company-model/references/workbook-profile.md), reconciled with the machine interface. [Company stage gates](research-cycle/references/company-sequence.md) define readiness; [research branches](research-cycle/references/research-branches.md) cover screening, macro, industry, historical and recurring work.

Use [foundational builds](company-model/references/foundational-builds.md) to construct locally readable forecasts from substantiated components. It includes generic inspection/build scripts, a blank plan and a synthetic Excel example.

Use the [twenty sector activity references](company-model/references/sectors/index.md) to choose the economic mechanism, evidence questions, operating build and sector-specific accounting/cash challenges. Each includes a synthetic executable miniature and blank scaffold; none contains issuer forecasts.

For early research use [fundamental/peer triage](theme-screening/references/screening-and-peer-triage.md): retain the universe, normalize relevant metrics and return a compact shortlist with next questions. [Thesis tests](valuation-thesis/references/thesis-tests.md) separate economic assumptions from market recognition, [historical replay](valuation-thesis/references/history-and-attribution.md) preserves the anchor and executable entry assumptions, and [recurring work items](monitor-review/references/recurring-research.md) let a fresh session resume from concrete artifacts. Provider, scheduler and UI implementation remain separate.

## Coverage and scope

Account for all bundled roles with the [coverage plan](research-cycle/references/research-coverage.md). Required work may reference eligible dated artifacts; conditional work needs an explicit applicability decision. Portfolio allocation, risk budgeting, strategy testing and non-company underwriting are [external workflows](research-cycle/references/scope-boundary.md). No scheduler or monitoring service is supplied.

## Handoffs and tools

Use the [earnings clock and peer read-through procedure](company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md) to relate latest-period results, subsequent-period expectations, earlier peer calls and upcoming catalysts to the research cutoff. [Provider retrieval](evidence-intake/references/research-data-providers.md) supports bounded requests to the configured data providers for fundamentals/expectations, market/actions/ownership, macro and meeting evidence, with explicit access/field limits. Non-English originals are retained and translated to the requested output language, English by default. The methodology has eighteen analytical and presentation specialists, with initiation and revision as two workflow entry skills.

Use the [shared handoff contract](research-cycle/references/handoffs.md) for identity, sources, claims, dependency versions, checks and next actions. Numerical helpers are optional and have documented input assumptions. Their examples are synthetic; passing structural or arithmetic checks does not establish financial completeness, source truth or investment performance.

Keep sibling skill folders together so relative links resolve. Load only the relevant specialist and references. Local skill files do not register tools, supply source access, activate monitoring or authorise external actions.
