# Company research skill index

Start at [research-cycle](research-cycle/SKILL.md), choose the [research mode](research-cycle/references/request-routing.md) and maintain the [sequence and coverage plan](research-cycle/references/research-coverage.md). The [repository guide](../README.md) lists all eighteen bundled skills, checks, default mandate and scope boundaries.

Use the [company sequence](research-cycle/references/company-sequence.md) for dependencies, [sector activity cards](company-model/references/sectors/index.md) for relevant mechanisms, [foundational builds](company-model/references/foundational-builds.md) for readable economic forecasts, and the [conviction record](investment-conviction/SKILL.md) for continuity across research vintages. Existing coverage uses [model-revision](model-revision/SKILL.md); new coverage uses [stock-initiation](stock-initiation/SKILL.md).

The catalog and coverage rules are authored guidance, not an automatic dispatcher. Perform required analysis or explicitly reuse valid dated work; consider conditional specialists and explain applicability. Incomplete evidence remains incomplete. Portfolio/trading services and active monitoring are [external](research-cycle/references/scope-boundary.md).
