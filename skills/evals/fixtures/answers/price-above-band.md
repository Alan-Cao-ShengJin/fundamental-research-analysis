# Expected outcome: price-above-band pack

Not for the agent. Graded by reading.

**Expected answer: mean reversion.** The price has gone from LC42 to LC60 while FY2027E consensus EPS moved from LC2.98 to LC3.00 and guidance, products, capacity and contracts are unchanged. The multiple has gone from about 14x to 20x. Under the house rule a temporary deviation from the band returns to the mean, and nothing in the pack is a structural change. Target 14.0 x LC3.00 = LC42.0, a price return of −30%, rating Sell. Dividend yield shown separately. Attractiveness is not applicable to a Sell; new money no.

**The market read the run should give.** The price-implied FY2027 EPS at the mean multiple is LC4.29, 43% above consensus; no read supports earnings at that level (revisions one up one down, guidance unchanged, no live line). The gap is a multiple move. The run must consider and reject the non-earnings explanations with the evidence given:

- Rates: the 10-year yield fell 30 basis points. For this exercise, assume the band has moved about one turn of P/E per 50 basis points of the 10-year yield (a synthetic assumption for grading, not a measured sensitivity); 30 basis points then supports well under one turn, not six.
- Flows: index inclusion on 1 September and passive ownership up five points. A flow explanation for part of the rise; it does not change earnings or the mean, and it can reverse.
- Takeover speculation: one unsourced press item and a no-comment. Not evidence of a bid; cannot carry a target.
- Peers: up 8% at 13 to 16x, so no sector re-rating to 20x.

**Fails.**

- Raising FY2027 or FY2028 forecasts toward the price-implied LC4.29 without an operating driver in the pack.
- Adopting a target multiple near 20x (a "persistent premium") on the strength of rates, flows or speculation; the pack supplies no structural change, so a persistent-premium conclusion fails.
- A Hold as a placeholder because the price is "already there", or a refusal to rate.
- Not naming the alternative explanations at all. The pass requires that rates, flows and the bid rumour were each considered and rejected on the evidence, with the observation that would change the view (a filed approach, an earnings-driven revision cycle, or a sustained estimate move) stated.

**Pass requires:** target within LC1 of 42.0, Sell, the market read above, the three alternatives considered and rejected, and the settling observation named.
