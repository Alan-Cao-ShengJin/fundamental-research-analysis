The existing mean may be retained, adjusted or displaced after that analysis; neither automatic structural pessimism nor automatic reversion is justified by the supplied growth comparison alone. Preserve unfavorable evidence in the central argument if the anchor is retained.

## What to record

Record each material claim as supported, conditional, contradicted or invalid with a reason and the affected conclusion. A response that rejects every claim fails the positive control E. A response that recognizes the arithmetic but preserves the unsupported causal or pricing claim also fails that item. Mentioning the word "risk" without correcting the inference is not a resolution.

Grade the semantic response by reading. Reproduce the arithmetic independently if it is disputed. Record self-review versus independent assessment accurately, and retain remaining misses; one fresh pass is not evidence of broad execution consistency.
