Fees relate to payments and account services. No material trading, insurance or foreign-currency business. About 82% of gross loans are secured; the remainder is unsecured consumer credit. The main competitors are other deposit-funded retail banks. Management identifies deposit repricing, lending spreads and credit losses as the principal earnings variables.
