# Evidence pack: Corvane Photonics (synthetic)

Everything here is invented for a research exercise; no company, customer, tracker, price or estimate is real. Use only this file as evidence. Research cutoff: Monday 9 November 2026. All money is US$ millions unless stated.

## The company

Corvane Photonics (ticker CRVN, Nasdaq) designs and makes indium phosphide (InP) lasers in its own fab in Oregon and assembles optical products in Malaysia. Fiscal years end on the Saturday nearest 30 September; FY2026 had 53 weeks and ended 3 October 2026. It reports four product lines:

- **Datacom lasers:** externally modulated lasers (EMLs) and continuous-wave (CW) lasers sold as chips to module makers for pluggable transceivers in data-centre networks.
- **Telecom components:** pump and tunable lasers for long-haul and metro networks. Mature; grows with carrier spending.
- **Optical circuit switches (OCS):** complete switch systems that redirect light between fibres. First revenue 1QFY26. One customer in production (Customer A, a cloud operator); a second in qualification.
- **Laser source modules:** external laser source modules that supply light to co-packaged optics (CPO) switches. First revenue 3QFY26, for Customer B's CPO switch platform.

Corvane does not make transceiver modules or switch chips.

## Management statements, 4QFY26 call (4 November 2026)

1. 1QFY27 guidance: revenue 370-390; non-GAAP EPS US$1.44-1.54.
2. "Datacom laser output is capacity-constrained through fiscal 2027. We run about 2,400 InP wafer starts a quarter; tool installs add 600 a quarter from the second fiscal quarter, and our new Ridgefield fab adds 1,500 a quarter from the fourth quarter of fiscal 2028. Each 1,000 wafer starts a quarter supports roughly $110 million of quarterly datacom laser revenue at today's mix and prices."
3. "Laser source modules use the same InP wafers: about 120 wafer starts a quarter for each $10 million of quarterly laser-source revenue."
4. "Customer B has told us its CPO switch platform ships in the second half of calendar 2027. Each laser source module lights 16 optical lanes, which is two 1.6T ports, at a price of about $240 a module. We are the lead source on that platform."
5. "Customer B signed a $900 million purchase commitment covering calendar 2027 through 2029, for laser source modules and datacom lasers. The product split is not specified. Amounts are non-cancellable once products pass qualification. Customer B prepaid $120 million on 20 October 2026."
6. "We expect to exit fiscal 2027 with OCS at an annualised revenue run-rate above $400 million. Customer A plans about 1,600 switches for 2027 deployments at roughly $250,000 each."
7. "Fiscal 2027 capital expenditure will be about $380 million, mostly Ridgefield."
8. "Our aspiration is $2.5 billion of revenue in fiscal 2028."
9. "Pluggable optics remain the large majority of ports through 2028. Scale-up links inside racks stay mostly copper for now; optical scale-up is a later opportunity we are sampling for."
