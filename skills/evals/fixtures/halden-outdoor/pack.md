Everything here is invented for a research exercise; no company, partner, price or estimate is real. Use only this file as evidence. Research cutoff: Monday 9 November 2026. Money is HK$ millions unless stated; the company reports in HK$.

## The company

Halden Outdoor Group (synthetic ticker HDOG, listed in Hong Kong, HK$ counter) sells outdoor apparel and footwear in Greater China through three brands. Financial year ends 31 December; it reports half-years and publishes quarterly retail-sales updates.

- **Halden:** the founding mass-market brand, 100% owned. About 60% of revenue.
- **Fjell:** premium technical outerwear, run through a joint venture 60% owned by Halden; the Nordic brand owner holds 40%.
- **Tundra:** premium hiking footwear, run through a joint venture 55% owned by Halden; a Japanese partner holds 45%.

The joint ventures are consolidated. A live-streaming sales channel launched in 2025 is part of the Halden brand and was about 2% of group revenue in 1H26.

## Management statements

- FY2024 results, published 31 March 2025 after the close: the Halden growth guide for 2025 was cut from "high single digit" to "low single digit". The shares fell 14% the next day.
- 1H25 results (26 August 2025): Halden guided "flat to slightly down" for 2H25.
- 1H26 results (25 August 2026): Halden operating margin guided at "about 18% for 2026, including about 2 points of local-government store subsidies that may not recur". Fjell and Tundra margins "stable". Store plan for 2026: Halden net closures of about 250; Fjell and Tundra about 45 and 40 net openings.
- 3Q26 operating update (15 October 2026): retail sales, year on year, Halden down mid-single digit; Fjell up 20-25%; Tundra up 25-30%. Management added: "Halden traded better in October and we believe the brand has stabilised." No October data was published.
- The Fjell joint-venture agreement runs to 2034; the partner may raise its royalty from 3% to 5% of Fjell revenue from 2028.
