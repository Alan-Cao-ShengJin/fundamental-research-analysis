# Research challenge

Review the thesis against raw evidence, calculations and relevant investment constraints. Identify what would change the decision; generic risk lists do not substitute for testing the actual claims.

For a company view, challenge the [conviction anchor](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md): is it decisive, economically connected to minority-owner returns and genuinely testable? Check the original wording, contrary evidence, missed milestones and independent deal breakers. A stable anchor cannot excuse changed funding/rights, an evidence error or a failed timetable; a changed price alone cannot prove it false. Apply the same scrutiny to an avoidance view, including legitimate transactions, corrected allegations and verified remediation. An alternative interpretation needs evidence; novelty or disagreement alone is not a reason to rewrite the prior view.

Use the [economic and market thesis tests](../valuation-thesis/references/thesis-tests.md) to separate a broken business assumption from weak recognition/timing. Challenge both where material: a rising price does not validate operating forecasts, and absent momentum does not by itself refute a funded value case. Require a supported expectations gap when claimed, rather than inventing disagreement to justify an edge.

For a standalone opinion, first restate its strongest fair interpretation and separate factual premises, forecast, causal mechanism, valuation implication and proposed action. Check each premise and identify the omitted assumption between adjacent links. A user asserting profit growth, for example, has not necessarily asserted an attractive stock return. Avoid inventing a stronger claim to refute.

Challenge [target underwriting](../valuation-thesis/references/target-underwriting.md): reconstruct the strongest explanation for the market price, test reasonable valuation assumptions, identify the actual analytical edge and show the no-repricing outcome. Apply this to derating as well as rerating. A scenario label or an earnings date cannot cure an unsupported convergence-based call.

First test whether the debate is the one [the market is trading](../research-cycle/references/what-the-market-trades.md): check it against the price-reaction log, the call Q&A and the revisions. A debate built from the filings can argue a year the backlog has settled, or model a legacy business while the price sits on a pipeline asset. Where our forecast for the live variable is below the price-implied figure, check that it starts from the latest run rate and capacity rather than from management's public plan, and that the report says why the live view is wrong. Then test each contested question from the stage-1 debate on its merits. State the strongest case for the side the thesis rejects, including the non-linear outcome if the question resolves its way (for example, growth that lasts for years rather than a window). Split that case into the claims that must hold, mark each supported, contradicted or untested, and name the evidence and timing that would settle it. Build the bear and bull scenarios from these debates resolving, with their effect on growth duration and the multiple, rather than from small moves in every input.

After the report and brief are drafted, read them as a second pass: test the material reasons for the call, each causal assertion, the valuation premise and the contrary evidence against the model and the study's evidence record, and send corrections back through the affected calculations before packaging. This pass adds to the earlier challenge of the thesis and model; it does not replace it.

For publication, perform that pass as the named fresh reader under the [bounded publication workflow](../report-layout/references/publication-review.md). Read the frozen files and original pivotal evidence, not only the author's summaries or defense. Assess the six writing dimensions, review the advisory number inventory, and complete the existing challenge handoff inside the publication packet with actual findings and applicable financial checks. Never auto-complete a review because the build passes. Recheck corrections; exhausted unresolved material findings leave a draft rather than an endless rewrite loop.

Apply the [inference review](references/inference-quality.md) to the claims on which the decision depends. Check what the cited observation actually measures, the assumptions connecting it to the conclusion, the strongest alternative explanation, and whether the proposed test distinguishes them. A source citation, completed Argument block or reproduced calculation does not establish that the inference follows. Record the pivotal links actually tested and their disposition in the existing challenge handoff; preserve a supported central judgment with its material uncertainty rather than forcing bullishness, pessimism or agreement with consensus.

Check that the reasons given for the call are the ones the target depends on. For each, compare how far the target moves if it fails under a defensible alternative (dependence) with how far our assumption differs from consensus (differentiation), holding the multiple and interacting drivers together. A reason that barely moves the target is support, not the case; a driver in line with consensus can still be the one the call depends on. When the stated reasons and the drivers that matter disagree, rewrite the argument, not the model.

## Challenge in five dimensions

1. **Evidence:** trace the decision-driving claims to accessible source locations and publication/availability dates. Check whether multiple sources are independent, quotations retain their qualifications and contrary evidence was considered. Missing or synthetic evidence cannot silently support a real-account claim.
2. **Economics:** explain the strongest alternative causal account. Is the proposed return actually market beta, a liquidity/credit premium, short-volatility risk or leverage? Why is it not already priced? What specifically could falsify the thesis?
3. **Numbers:** reproduce key unit/period/currency conversions, statement bridges, scenario arithmetic and valuation basis. Check whether adjustments are motivated by evidence or by the desired conclusion. Do not treat a zero check or a numerical round trip as economic validation.
4. **Testing:** inspect point-in-time availability, selection/survivorship bias, alignment, cost assumptions, sample size, repeated parameter search and reuse of a holdout. A held-out correlation check is not proof of tradable spread convergence.
5. **Implementation and portfolio:** challenge exact contract mappings, fill/legging assumptions, margin pools, collateral, liquidity in stress, pending actions and overlap with existing risks. Ask how the strategy can fail even when the underlying thesis is right.

Use issue classifications: invalid fact/calculation, critical missing evidence, sensitive assumption, implementation blocker, or nonblocking uncertainty. Materiality depends on the mandate and decision, not an arbitrary count or severity score.

For a company recommendation, challenge the [governance assessment](../corporate-governance/SKILL.md): is apparent cheapness compensation for weak rights, cash diversion or poor allocation, and what could actually change it? Check that the same risk is not charged multiple times. Test the pivotal investment insight against attributed/price-implied expectations and a credible cash-return or repricing mechanism; do not assume the market is unaware of a known fact.

Challenge the target and the separate new-money judgment under the [decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md). Test downside, costs, horizon and opportunity cost. For multiple changes, challenge the evidence and why investors would reassess the company; a results date or peer discount alone is insufficient. Revise unsupported targets rather than adding a disclaimer.

Challenge the [valuation driver](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-driver-and-dividends.md): does a dividend narrative actually support the chosen yield, payout and cash coverage, or merely disguise an unexplained P/E? Check policy adoption, earnings-year versus payment-year payout, already-ex dates, recurring cash and higher-capex/cut scenarios. Re-expressing a P/E as a yield is not independent validation.

Check the [decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md): rating, price return beside it, the gap to consensus, realisable target, quote/period basis and same-horizon bear/bull cases. Reconcile method disagreements. Business quality, a low multiple or polished presentation alone does not establish a Buy.

Before a severe governance/reporting conclusion, apply the [red-flag review](../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md): reconstruct pivotal evidence, check corrections/remediation and plausible benign explanations, separate severity from confidence and test double-counted losses. An audit qualification, low cash yield or allegation needs its exact scope; no ratio is a fraud finding. Equally, management reassurance, cheapness or a favorable aggregate score cannot dismiss a substantive threat. Review the same issue IDs and state what is bounded, resolved or still essential. A second self-review is not independent assurance.

## Resolve and hand off

For company recommendations, apply the [underwriting standard](../research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md), [investor judgment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md) and [empirical/source validation](../research-cycle/references/empirical-research-and-validation.md). Select tests by decision materiality: critical assumptions/source credibility, competitive response and funded marginal returns, advantage duration, shareholder access, priced expectations, unchanged-multiple returns, joint downside and probability versus payoff. A high win rate or attractive upside/downside ratio alone is insufficient; a plausible losing case does not automatically block a defensible central call.

Test [market-context/narrative repricing](../macro-event-analysis/references/market-regime-and-investor-base.md) when it contributes to the thesis, with no automatic priority over other dimensions. Examine credible association, what is priced in and persistence/fade without requiring named buyers or proven inflows. Deepen access/capacity/supply checks when they are themselves decisive. Keep operating exposure, attention, holdings and measured flows distinct.

Reconstruct pivotal claims from original evidence, reproduce important arithmetic and retain contrary/failed analogues. Record what was actually checked; repetition is not independent evidence, a second self-review is not an independent review, and drafted evaluation cases are not passed behavioral tests. A standalone opinion challenge may be a self-review. Full initiations and material model changes require the fresh pre-writing model checkpoint in the company sequence; host capability and actual execution must be reported honestly.

For an opinion-only request, return the reconstructed claim, fact checks, logical gaps, strongest contrary evidence, failure scenarios and tests that would settle them. Classify individual claims as supported, contradicted or unresolved according to the evidence. Include evidence strengthening the claim when found; challenge does not require reaching an adverse answer. State access gaps rather than fabricating counterexamples. This mode does not add a final stock rating.

Return the strongest counter-case, reproduced checks, unresolved issues, evidence that would settle each issue, and which upstream/downstream artifacts must be revised. State which issues block a candidate and which permit further paper observation.

Use a `challenge` [handoff](../research-cycle/references/handoffs.md) for both standalone and full-cycle reviews, with stable claim IDs and explicit upstream versions.

After a material change, rerun the affected calculation and downstream interpretation. Do not mark an issue resolved merely because wording was softened. Preserve rejected cases and failed tests so the next cycle does not rediscover them.

If the result is no action, propose the next useful investigation or an observable watch with a review date. Do not force a bullish/bearish conclusion to satisfy a return ambition. This review does not approve any proposal or attest that a model's statements are all true.
