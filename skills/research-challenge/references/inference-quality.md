# Test whether the evidence supports the investment inference

Use for the material arguments in a thesis and its final report or brief. Work within the existing challenge handoff; this is not a new report template, completion score or requirement to challenge every immaterial sentence. Apply the same standard to favorable and adverse conclusions.

## Reconstruct the link

For each decision-driving conclusion, identify the observation, its actual definition, the calculation, the assumption connecting it to the conclusion, and the financial implication. A quotation can establish what management said without establishing that its forecast will happen. A ratio can be arithmetically correct without measuring the economic mechanism claimed. Source and exhibit IDs establish traceability, not inference validity.

Ask what else could produce the observation. Use the strongest economically plausible alternative, then identify evidence that distinguishes it from the preferred explanation. Merely repeating the original fact is not a discriminating test. Select the best-supported central interpretation; incomplete identification does not require paralysis or a conservative default. State material qualifications where they affect the conclusion, not only in a remote risk section.

## Apply the relevant test

| Argument | Test before relying on it |
| --- | --- |
| A company or unit looks cheaper than peers | Match asset/income boundaries, ownership, period, accounting and financing. Show how adjustments affect the ordering. Follow [peer valuation](../../valuation-thesis/references/method-selection.md#peer-valuation); a residual operating multiple is not directly a whole-equity multiple. |
| A rising unit ratio proves stronger demand or productivity | Check numerator scope, average denominator, channel/mix, entrants/exits, acquisitions and seasonality. Separate a true operating change from composition effects under the [driver measurement tests](../../company-model/references/revenue-projection.md#test-what-a-driver-actually-measures). |
| Similar growth justifies the old valuation | Assess the duration and quality of that growth, reinvestment, cash, rates, financing and shareholder rights. Explain why relevant differences do or do not change the regime. Similarity on one variable cannot settle the whole comparison. |
| Several valuation methods confirm the target | Trace their inputs. Distinguish a separately supported anchor from a target-implied input and disclose shared assumptions. Mathematical agreement can be useful reconciliation without independent confirmation. |
| One transaction's loss bounds governance risk | Separate the measured loss from what the process and incentives imply for future allocation and access to value. Assess constraints and remediation; do not infer either a permanently bad controller or a harmless one-off from price movement alone. |
| A scenario or probability demonstrates attractive risk/reward | Resolve the adverse debate jointly through operations and the applicable valuation mechanism. Test omitted material tails. A band offset or weights summing to one do not establish adequate coverage or justified probabilities. |
| The price can only mean one thing | List the other assumptions held fixed in the residual calculation and vary plausible alternatives. Describe a conditional implication rather than claiming to observe a unique investor belief. |
| An aggregate forecast requires a specific driver assumption | Construct a feasible counterexample outside that asserted condition, keeping other inputs within supported ranges. A single valid witness refutes necessity. Apply the [bounded-bridge guidance](../../company-model/references/benchmark-comparison.md#a-conditional-bridge-is-not-a-unique-explanation); an illustrative single-driver bridge does not identify consensus components. |
| A known payment or obligation establishes its classification and timing | Trace the [separate states](../../company-model/references/statement-forecast.md#classification-recognition-and-payment-states): observed amount, attribution, recognition, settlement and applicable constraint. Challenge unsupported transitions and distinguish management goals from mandatory deadlines. |
| Every comparison supports a conclusion | Define the tested set, check its members and use the computed count. Selected rows cannot establish a universal claim. |
| Company results show an industry shift | Keep the observation within its evidence population, or label a wider conclusion as a reasoned, qualified inference. Independent category evidence can support a wider observation. |
| A cash yield belongs to shareholders | Reconcile economic non-owner interests and access restrictions with the capital denominator. Paid minority dividends alone do not establish owner cash; wholly owned cash needs no automatic haircut. |
| A reviewer proposes a corrected number | Recalculate it from traced inputs and use the checked rounding in the finding. Review authority does not establish numerical correctness. |
| A remaining-period forecast lies outside an annual guide | Reconcile the known period and remaining numerator/denominator to the full year before judging the guide. Compare quantity, dates, unit, currency/scale, perimeter and earnings basis; apply the same checks to reviewer corrections. |
| A strong cumulative result describes the latest period | Derive material incremental flows from comparable cumulative disclosures where available; distinguish the latest period, the cumulative result and a known reversal. Ratios, changing-share EPS and balance-sheet stocks need appropriate bridges. |

Use business-appropriate measures. An industrial cash adjustment is not a bank liquidity analysis, and positive-EPS multiples cannot be imposed on a loss-making company. A predictable distributor can have a supported yield-based case without a P/E band or discrete catalyst. A cyclical can have higher P/E on lower earnings and a falling price. The requirement is coherent economics, not a fixed direction for a ratio.

## Resolve the finding

- **Supported:** the observation and calculation fit the claimed scope and the material alternative has been addressed. This supports the stated conclusion, not certainty about the outcome.
- **Conditional:** the conclusion depends on an explicit, reasoned assumption or an unresolved distinction. Retain a defensible central forecast, quantify its sensitivity where useful and narrow the wording to what is supported.
- **Contradicted or invalid:** a material source, comparison, period or inference does not support the claim. Correct the affected model/valuation or remove the unsupported implication; adding a citation or softening one word is not a resolution.

Use these dispositions in the existing claim rationale/checks; do not add undeclared handoff fields. The report renderer's supported/contradicted/untested labels remain analyst judgments, not automated findings. Recheck both the report and brief after corrections: a claim can be qualified correctly in the full report and overstated in the headline or brief.

Keep the chosen target independent of the desired call. If it remains justified after contrary evidence, explain why; if not, recompute the affected target and rating. A review that disagrees with the prior conclusion is as valid as one that strengthens it. Preserve what was actually tested for the next cycle.

For a relative-quality claim, inspect evidence for both sides on compatible periods and definitions. High own margins or returns do not establish superiority to an unmeasured peer. Remove an unsupported comparison or obtain the missing evidence; neither action automatically requires a discount or a different call. Check that the actual valuation anchor, not merely the displayed ratio, uses the selected target convention, including scenarios and sensitivity repricing.

## Calibration

Use the existing publication [claim-support checks](../../report-layout/references/claim-support.md) for applicable deterministic parts. Source populations, rights and qualifications still require substantive judgment. "On our inference" does not make an illustrative consensus bridge the only route to those earnings; use necessity language only when alternatives have been ruled out.
