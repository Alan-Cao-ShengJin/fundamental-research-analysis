# Verify the scope and arithmetic of material claims

Use within publication review, not as another research stage. `coverage.claim-support` is `checked` with `assertionIds` covering the relevant `claimAssertions`, or `not-applicable` with a reason and no assertions. Undecided coverage blocks publication. The reader determines completeness: a missing assertion does not establish that a claim is absent.

Each assertion has `id`, `claimId` (a recorded handoff claim or reviewer finding), `location`, `sourceIds` and `rationale`. Use the [generic field examples](../assets/claim-assertions.example.json) and `claim_checks.py` through `publication_review.py check`. The strict shared handoff envelope is unchanged.

## Bound inputs

A binding is `{"model":"Sheet!D10"}`, `{"modelFormula":"=AVERAGE('History'!D10:D21)"}`, or `{"file":"data/comparisons.json","pointer":"/rows/0/return"}`. Data files must already be frozen inputs; add `reviewInputs` before preparation when necessary. Model formulas read the specification already checked against the recalculated workbook without modifying it. Bindings do not validate extraction or metric choice.

## Applicable checks

- **Quantified set:** `kind: quantified-set`, unique `members` (`id`, bound `value`), `predicate` (`op`, numeric `threshold`), `quantifier: all|any|none`, `scope: listed|universe`. Universe claims also bind a complete `universeIds` list from frozen JSON and state `universeDefinition`. Missing members and false quantifiers fail. Use the computed count and actual scope in prose: selected rows do not establish every possible window. The reader checks the population definition and omitted relevant alternatives.
- **Evidence population:** `kind: evidence-scope`, `sourceScopes` mapping registered IDs to `unit|company|sample|industry`, and `conclusionScope`. Company-only evidence cannot be a reported industry fact. Wider hypotheses remain permissible with an inference/assumption handoff basis, a rationale and explicit `limitations`. Keep those qualifications in the report and brief. A multi-company sample is not automatically the whole industry; relevant industry evidence can support an industry observation. Source representativeness remains reviewed.
- **Whose cash:** `kind: cash-boundary` binds `numerator`, `denominator`, `ratio`; declares `nonControllingInterests: none|present|unknown`, `numeratorBoundary: consolidated|parent-economic|parent-available`, `denominatorBoundary: parent-equity|group-capital|parent-distributions`, and `interpretation: owner-return|consolidated-proxy|distribution-coverage`. Owner return with other interests requires a bound `reconciliation` of `baseCash - nonOwnerEconomicCash - unavailableCash`, with `rightsBasis`. Paid partner dividends need not equal economic entitlement; retained parent-owned JV cash need not be accessible. Do not charge restrictions twice or haircut wholly owned cash automatically. A limited consolidated proxy can remain a proxy; coverage uses distributions and its access assumptions. Select business-appropriate measures rather than imposing industrial FCF on financial institutions.
- **Author/reviewer calculation:** `kind: calculation`, `role: author|reviewer`, bound named `inputs`, arithmetic `expression` using those names and `+ - * /`, numeric `shown`, and `decimals` (0-6). The checker reproduces arithmetic and display rounding, rejects unbound replacement constants and executable expressions. New targets and break-even corrections proposed by reviewers need verification too. Put the computed result in the actual finding; the ledger cannot detect contradictory free text elsewhere.

## Feasible bridges and actual displayed figures

`kind: bounded-bridge` binds an `offset`, `target` and `drivers` (`id`, numeric `coefficient`, bound `low`, bound `high`), with boolean `expectedFeasible` and a `domainRationale` explaining matched units, independence and omitted constraints. It tests one affine equality over the declared box and reports its achievable interval and witness. For a necessity claim, test the domain where that claimed condition does not hold. A feasible witness refutes necessity; infeasibility applies only to that declared domain. Do not turn a source range into an independent joint assumption when economics couple its drivers, or apply this linear helper to products or changing denominators without a valid transformation. See [bridge interpretation](../../company-model/references/benchmark-comparison.md#a-conditional-bridge-is-not-a-unique-explanation).

Bind material calculated/source figures in prose as well as the existing financial-assertion families. `kind: rendered-measure` has bound `values` (one scalar or two range endpoints) and nonempty `displays`. Each display names the exact frozen HTML `document`, span `id`, `period`, `unit`, `style: number|percent|multiple`, `decimals: 0..6`, `transform: identity|negate|magnitude` and `order: input|ascending`. A `calculation` assertion can use the same `displays` to bind its **unrounded computed result**, rather than another manually typed result. Do not bind the rounded `shown` as though it were an independent calculation.

Render with `bound_measure(id, values, period, unit, style=..., decimals=..., transform=..., order=...)` from `report_layout`. One formatter controls raw values, sign transformation, range order and visible text. For example, signed cash-flow ratios remain negative inputs; a positive payout display explicitly uses `magnitude`. A negative economic return retains `identity`. Do not apply absolute values universally. Currency labels or explanatory words can sit outside the span. Ranges show both endpoints with units. The [generic display assertion](../assets/rendered-measures.example.json) is original and industry-neutral.

The checker verifies the declared transformation, both endpoints and **every matching occurrence in each named document** against bound values. An identical number elsewhere is not a binding. Newly formatted spans must be covered by a calculation or rendered-measure assertion. Legacy `bound_value` remains supported for the existing financial families; the new formatter is available for general prose/ranges. Omitted material figures, misleading surrounding prose and source interpretation still require the reader. This is stronger binding, not automatic semantic validation of every number in a PDF.

## Comparable definitions and incremental flows

Use `kind: comparison` or `incremental-flow`, with the same common assertion fields and a `role: author|reviewer`. Each side binds a `value` and a `definition`: `quantity`, `unit`, `currency` (`none` where appropriate), `scale`, `perimeter`, `basis`, and `measure: flow|ratio|per-share|stock`. Describe units and scale explicitly; the checker cannot infer them from a number. Non-stock measures use ISO `start` and exclusive `end`; stocks use `asOf`.

`basis` describes the accounting/earnings definition, not whether the observation is actual or estimated: a forecast can be compared with an actual on the same definition while remaining labelled as a forecast.

A `comparison` has `left`, `right`, numeric ordering `op` (`>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=`, `==`) and `relation`:

- `same-period` requires matching definitions and dates. A remaining-nine-month margin is not directly comparable with a full-year guide.
- `matched-periods` permits different dated periods of comparable duration (up to seven days' difference for fiscal calendars), with an `alignment` rationale. Other definition fields stay matched.
- `cross-section` additionally permits different entity perimeters, with an `alignment` rationale. It does not permit an unconverted currency/scale difference or silently different earnings definitions. The reader decides whether the comparison is economically useful.
- `contextual` permits explicitly descriptive comparisons across durations, entities or accounting definitions, with `alignment`, `limitations` and `interpretation: descriptive-only`. Units, currency/scale, quantity and measure still match. For example a quarterly margin can be shown beside the prior full-year margin as context, or reported and adjusted peer ratios shown directionally. This is not evidence that a remaining-period forecast breaches an annual guide or that underlying relative quality is proven. The reader checks that the prose retains this limited interpretation; do not use a contextual label to evade a necessary like-for-like test.

When normalization is necessary, a side may contain `reconcile`: bound named `inputs`, `expression`, bound `result`, resulting `definition`, and `rationale`. `original` is reserved for the raw side's value and must participate in the arithmetic. For example, reconcile a remaining-period margin with known-period profit and revenues into a full-year margin before comparing the annual guide. Reproduce currency, ownership or accounting-basis transformations where appropriate; do not merely rename the original period. See the original, industry-neutral [field examples](../assets/comparable-claims.example.json).

An `incremental-flow` has `current`, `prior`, `incremental`. All three definitions must describe the same additive flow. Current/prior cumulative periods share a start; the increment covers `[prior.end, current.end)` and must equal current less prior. Do not subtract cumulative ratios, changing-share EPS or balance-sheet stocks as additive flows. Reconcile numerator/denominator, profit/shares, stock movements, changed perimeters or restatements explicitly before making the relevant comparison. If the inputs are missing or not comparable, document that instead of inventing data.

`coverage.comparisons` covers all declared comparison IDs; `coverage.incremental-flows` covers all declared incremental-flow IDs. Each is `checked` with exactly those `assertionIds`, or `not-applicable` with a substantive reason and no hidden declared assertions. For `researchMode: earnings-update`, incremental coverage also needs `candidates`: unique `id`, boolean `material`, registered `sourceIds`, `reason`, and `status: checked|unavailable|not-comparable|immaterial`. A checked candidate names its `assertionId`; every incremental assertion must be accounted for. Material candidates cannot be dismissed as immaterial. Explain genuine absence of comparable flows with a sourced unavailable/noncomparable candidate and reasoned non-applicability. The reader establishes that the inventory includes the material flows; a model cannot discover a source the author never entered.

## Limits

Checks apply to declared claims. They cannot authenticate evidence, discover every omitted assertion, prove a population representative, or guarantee that rendered wording matches a ledger's scope. Argument-support and source/financial-basis review remain necessary. Do not ban words such as "all" or "industry", add conservative haircuts without evidence, or alter forecasts merely to pass.
