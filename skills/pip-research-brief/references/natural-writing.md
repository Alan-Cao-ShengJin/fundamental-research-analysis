# Edit research prose for the reader

Apply after the analysis and figures are settled, before rendering a report or delivering a research answer. Use the user's voice sample when supplied; otherwise write as a concise professional analyst addressing an informed investor. Return the edited result, without publishing drafts or an editorial critique unless requested.

This guide adapts the editing principles in [Humanizer v3.1.0](https://github.com/blader/humanizer/blob/0ef9fe5bc3572734fd3ae6c448fe00a30cd5dcf0/SKILL.md), Copyright (c) 2025 Siqi Chen, MIT licensed. It is integrated into this skill; no separate installation is required.

## Edit meaning and structure

- State the decision and economic reason first. Replace abstract praise with a specific change in sales, costs, cash, shareholder rights or value.
- Remove introductory announcements, invented objections, rhetorical question-and-answer openings, dramatic endings and sentences that repeat the heading or preceding paragraph.
- Give each material fact one main home. Use a caption to explain a chart's implication; do not narrate every plotted value again. Repeating the call/target in a brief and its separate main report is useful; repeating the same argument throughout one report is not.
- Replace generic authority with the named source and its actual claim. Distinguish what management said from what the model assumes.
- Use direct verbs and natural sentence lengths. Cut formulaic contrasts, lists padded to three items, unnecessary dashes, strings of qualifiers and inflated adjectives. Keep a comparison when it answers a real investment question.
- Use headings that name the subject. Reserve bold for the decision, key figures and a few phrases needed for scanning. Use lists for genuinely parallel information.

Do not invent personality, firsthand experience, anecdotes or certainty to make the writing sound human. Financial research should sound clear and candid. Punctuation and individual words are editing signals, not proof of authorship; a mechanical blacklist can damage precise terminology.

## Preserve the investment meaning

Protect the call, reference price/time, target/date, scenario prices, signs, units, currencies, reporting scope, citations and actual/forecast boundary. Keep the inclusive +/-10% Hold rule. Preserve distinctions between price and total return, profit and cash, statutory and adjusted earnings, group and parent claims, and attainable target versus intrinsic value.

Remove repeated hedges while retaining uncertainty that changes the decision. A forecast remains conditional. A governance allegation does not become a finding. An unavailable series does not become evidence of absence. A shorter sentence must not imply a causal relationship that the source did not establish.

A prose-only edit must not change model inputs, formulas, data, code, identifiers or link targets. If editing exposes a factual problem, resolve it through the evidence/model workflow and regenerate affected outputs.

## Final pass

Read the report as an investor deciding whether the call is credible. Remove any sentence that adds neither a relevant fact, a necessary inference, an assumption nor a decision consequence. Move useful supporting detail to the linked evidence or appendix; retain material risks and qualifications in the main report. Check the edited figures, sources and scenario logic against the current decision record. Deliver only the final prose.
