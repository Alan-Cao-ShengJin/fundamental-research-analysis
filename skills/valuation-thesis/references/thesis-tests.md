# Economic thesis and market evidence

Use when forming or challenging a name-specific thesis. Keep one versioned thesis in the existing company package, linked to its evidence, model and price cutoff. This complements the valuation and risk work; it is not a separate recommendation gate.

## Make the proposition testable

State the claim, horizon and shareholder-return mechanism in a sentence. Identify the few assumptions on which the case depends. For each material assumption retain the model metric/period, supported central estimate and range, source, observable confirming or disconfirming evidence and the action a new observation would trigger. Label analyst thresholds and distinguish invalidation, an ordinary miss, time expiry and price-risk limits.

Separate two questions without imposing equal weight or making market confirmation a prerequisite:

| Question | Evidence and test | What it can change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the business and valuation case hold? | Unit/segment economics, competitive response, funding, common-share access, price-implied assumptions, properly dated consensus and a credible counter-case | Operating forecasts, cash/distributions, intrinsic-value range and supported target |
| Does the market evidence support recognition within this horizon? | Chain/peer-relative performance, sector conditions, breadth, attention, documented ownership/flows and a plausible catalyst | Timing, attainable valuation and recognition/no-recognition scenarios; operating assumptions only through an economic channel |

Anchor comparisons in a documented economic peer/chain set. If no map exists, sketch only what is needed for the question. Do not turn every name into a full thematic scan. Compare against the market as well as relevant peers when market confirmation matters; a rising share price alone cannot distinguish issuer-specific evidence from broad beta or FX.

## Describe the expectations gap honestly

Name the difference between the analyst case, observed price-implied conditions and attributable published expectations. Explain its evidence, magnitude and per-share consequence. A peer discount, broker target spread or neglected name does not establish mispricing. If no differentiated fundamental view is supported, say so and assess the available cash-return/valuation case without inventing a consensus mistake. Do not equate disagreement with edge or declare a crowded trade merely because many articles repeat it.

A business can be attractive before the price confirms it, or richly valued despite strong market confirmation. Describe that distinction when useful; do not force a left/right entry label, favor momentum by default or require identified future buyers for a defensible central case. Use the shared rating/attractiveness rules after validating the target. A deterioration in market context can change timing or the target scenario without falsifying the business claim; operating delivery can also falsify a thesis despite a rising price.

## Keep the thesis usable after publication

Retain the strongest counter-case, load-bearing economic assumptions, material market recognition conditions and specific next observations. Link them to the [review work item](../../monitor-review/references/recurring-research.md), model dependency and prior vintage. Update the living note when evidence changes, preserving the original expectation and what was knowable then. A no-change result is valid. Do not silently rewrite an old thesis to match the outcome or activate a schedule just by listing follow-up conditions.
