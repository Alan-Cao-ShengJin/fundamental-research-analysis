# Initiate stock coverage

Establish whether this security offers an attractive payoff at the reference price. Use the full company sequence in [research cycle](../research-cycle/SKILL.md), with [company model](../company-model/SKILL.md) supplying the financial construction. A request only to build a financial model can use that specialist without producing a stock recommendation.

## Establish the initial case

Create the initiation [coverage plan](../research-cycle/references/research-coverage.md), then reconcile its required stages and conditional branches at completion. This includes business/competition, sector-guided modeling, early risk reviews, the three financial-quality passes, conviction and next observations; do not silently omit a useful specialist because it is absent from the main stage table.

- Define the issuer/share class, accounting basis, question, horizon and evidence universe. Check existing work to distinguish first coverage from an update. Honor an explicitly independent/fresh-study request; do not silently inherit its prior assumptions or verdict.
- Investigate business economics, industry structure, customers, competition and moat; establish relevant historical base rates and a credible shareholder-return mechanism. Separate management ambition, market expectations and the analyst's case.
- Select applicable [sector economic mechanisms](../company-model/references/sectors/index.md) to guide the initial source questions, operating breakdown and relevant accounting/valuation checks. Material mixed activities can need different builds; do not import every KPI from a sector label.
- Build source-backed historical statements and operating drivers. Complete initial governance and financial-red-flag reviews before forecasts, then build revenue/costs and integrated IS/CF/BS. Use the [company sequence](../research-cycle/references/company-sequence.md) for dependencies and readiness.
- Select valuation methods from the business economics. Audit the model, test implied expectations and minority shareholder access, challenge the central case, then conclude under the shared [decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md).
- Freeze the initial evidence, assumptions, model/run identity, forecast, valuation and decision. Identify pivotal assumptions and observable revision conditions for future reviews.

Develop the [conviction anchor](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) during investigation and finalize it after model/risk/challenge work: one decisive economic or avoidance proposition, supporting/contrary evidence, owner-cash mechanism, intended horizon, milestones and what would change the view. Preserve it with the initial case so future reviews can test the same proposition rather than rewrite the story around price movements.

An available prior model may supply permitted observations or useful structure; its forecast and conclusion require examination. If the requested independent study excludes prior work, preserve that boundary.

## Skill emphasis and output

Use evidence intake, company modeling, governance, financial quality, valuation, challenge and investment conclusion across the material company case. Theme, macro, management questions and portfolio work remain conditional. Existing uncertainty and rating rules apply.

The output explains the business, competitive economics, supported investment insight, model/valuation, counter-case and what would change the view. Deliver the [human-auditable Excel forecast model](../company-model/references/workbook-profile.md) with the initial coverage package, alongside any machine interface. Use [research brief](../research-brief/SKILL.md) for the initial pitch and its decision brief, matching the requested format and length. A source folder or earnings summary alone is not an initiation.

For existing coverage, follow the governing [request routing](../research-cycle/references/request-routing.md) into a regular review or major earnings/catalyst update through [model revision](../model-revision/SKILL.md). Invalid or missing foundations can require targeted rebuilding; identify that scope before calling the analysis complete.
