# Initiate stock coverage

Establish whether this security offers an attractive payoff at the reference price. Use the full company sequence in [research cycle](../research-cycle/SKILL.md), with [company model](../company-model/SKILL.md) supplying the financial construction. A request only to build a financial model can use that specialist without producing a stock recommendation.

## Establish the initial case

- Define the issuer/share class, accounting basis, question, horizon and evidence universe. A new initiation is a fresh study: work only from the [approved sources](../evidence-intake/references/research-data-providers.md#approved-sources) and the skills, and do not inherit earlier research or model assumptions. Documents explicitly supplied for this request, including a host-curated inbox pack, are authorized evidence; respect its boundary. Do not browse unrelated local files. Use the existing-coverage route only when the user asks to update coverage. For a dual-listed issuer, choose the rated line before collecting data; price, band, peers and consensus all follow it.
- Work through the [world state at the research date](../research-cycle/references/world-state.md) and [what the market is trading](../research-cycle/references/what-the-market-trades.md) before setting the debate. Public filings are available to every investor; the case rests on a judgment about the variable and period that set the price now, and on whether the market has read those filings correctly. Settle any development that changes what sets the price before the model and valuation are built.
- Before modeling, list the past year's results-call Q&A or management meeting remarks and the covering brokers' notes over the same period, then read in full the few that could change the call, [spending paid credits with judgment](../evidence-intake/references/research-data-providers.md#spend-alphapai-credits-with-judgment); a list of titles or an excerpt is not a read. Record what could not be opened and identify the speaker, date and source status. A fixed evidence pack limits that search; report gaps rather than query outside its boundary. Broker notes supply company-disclosed detail and forecasts to bridge against under [evidence intake](../evidence-intake/SKILL.md#build-the-evidence-pack); the house forecast stays our own.
- Run [industry analysis](../industry-analysis/SKILL.md) before the operating forecasts: industry growth, the company's competitive niche, whether entry barriers are rising or falling, upstream costs and downstream customer behaviour. Use its findings to set growth, share, price, cost and margin-duration assumptions, rather than extending the company's reported history. Establish historical base rates and a credible shareholder-return mechanism. Separate management ambition, market expectations and the analyst's case.
- Select applicable [sector economic mechanisms](../company-model/references/sectors/index.md) to guide the initial source questions, operating breakdown and relevant accounting/valuation checks. Material mixed activities can need different builds; do not import every KPI from a sector label.
- Build source-backed historical statements and operating drivers. Complete initial governance and financial-red-flag reviews before forecasts, then build revenue/costs and integrated IS/CF/BS. Use the [company sequence](../research-cycle/references/company-sequence.md) for dependencies and readiness.
- Select valuation methods from the business economics. Audit the model, test implied expectations and minority shareholder access, challenge the central case, then conclude under the shared [decision contract](../investment-conclusion/references/decision-contract.md).
- Freeze the initial evidence, assumptions, model/run identity, forecast, valuation and decision. Identify pivotal assumptions and observable revision conditions for future reviews.

Draft the [conviction anchor](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) during investigation and finalize it after model, risk and challenge work, so future reviews test the same proposition rather than rewrite the story around price movements.

Use the [sample model](../company-model/assets/company-model-example.xlsx) for readable sequencing, links and depth. Choose the actual financial structure from the issuer's economics and relevant sector guidance; take evidence from sources authorized for this study.

## Skill emphasis and output

Use evidence intake, company modeling, governance, financial quality, valuation, challenge and investment conclusion across the material company case. Theme, macro, management questions and portfolio work remain conditional. Existing uncertainty and rating rules apply.

The output explains the business, the industry and its effect on the forecasts, competitive economics, supported investment insight, model/valuation, counter-case and what would change the view. Deliver the [human-auditable Excel forecast model](../company-model/references/workbook-profile.md), built to the [model standard](../company-model/references/model-standard.md), with the initial coverage package, alongside any machine interface. Use [research brief](../research-brief/SKILL.md) for the initial pitch and its decision brief, matching the requested format and length. Deliver the full report, the one-page brief and the Excel model together in the study's deliverables folder and, once the fresh review completes, as the release zip under the publication workflow below; the report lists the model and brief as accompanying files (`Report.companions`). A source folder or earnings summary alone is not an initiation. A request for fewer deliverables trims what is delivered, not the stages behind it.

For existing coverage, follow the governing [request routing](../research-cycle/references/request-routing.md) into a regular review or major earnings/catalyst update through [model revision](../model-revision/SKILL.md). Invalid or missing foundations can require targeted rebuilding; identify that scope before calling the analysis complete.

Use the [bounded publication workflow](../report-layout/references/publication-review.md): freeze analysis for a fresh writer, include model-linked summary statements in the full report, and obtain a fresh reader's substantive review before packaging the exact reviewed files. A successful draft render is not a released initiation.
