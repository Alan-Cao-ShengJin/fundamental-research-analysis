# Evidence and underwriting standard

Use this standard for a full company recommendation and material revisions. Apply only the relevant tests to a focused question. A deterministic model run establishes calculation integrity; investment readiness additionally requires supported economics, shareholder access to value and a defensible return path.

Apply [investor judgment under imperfect information](investor-judgment.md) when selecting evidence, interpreting management/sell-side views and estimating future performance. Reasoned inference and educated guesses are normal inputs. Seek a defensible central case and attractive prospective return/risk at the price, without requiring certainty or a positive return in every plausible scenario.

## Integrate the analysis by materiality

Conversation order, document position and the date a technique was added do not establish analytical importance. Assess business economics/competition, financial quality and funded growth, governance/shareholder access, valuation/expectations, macro/market narratives and probability/payoff as connected dimensions. Choose depth and emphasis by this security's economics, price, horizon and decision sensitivity, without fixed weights or equal space for every dimension. A decisive governance, liquidity, funding or market-context issue can dominate a particular case; no dimension dominates every case by default.

The research sequence expresses information dependencies, not a ranking of importance. Evidence and context may develop together; a preliminary hypothesis can guide collection, while the final call follows modeling, valuation and challenge. Reuse the same observations across passes, preserve uncertainty and avoid counting one economic effect repeatedly. Select the pivotal thesis because of its explanatory and decision value, not because it was the latest topic raised.

## Red-flag risk triage

Assess severe governance threats to minority claims early and as high-priority concerns: value controlled by others may never reach outside holders. Run the [financial-red-flag procedure](../../pip-financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md) before forecasts, deepen findings in model audit and revisit them during challenge. Reporting/fraud signals are normally secondary to that governance emphasis, but core cash/asset reliability or solvency can independently decide the case. This is a risk-triage preference, not fixed analytical weights or a requirement to lead every report with risks.

Separate priority, confidence and disposition; test benign business/accounting explanations before alleging misconduct. High cash, high debt or missing normally private confirmations alone prove nothing. A cheap multiple or aggregate score cannot offset a critical threat. Carry bounded risks into model/valuation scenarios without double-counting. Essential unbounded baseline problems limit the dependent conclusion; ordinary uncertainty and disclosed remediated issues do not automatically block a call.

## Decision-driving assumptions

Apply the [earnings clock and peer read-through procedure](../../pip-company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md): latest reported period/call, next-period expectations, elapsed time and upcoming dated/estimated catalysts. Within near-term earnings analysis, current delivery and the next outlook receive more attention than older periods; history still calibrates seasonality, cycles and credibility. Preserve pre-event baselines and source availability before inferring peer signals, surprises or what may be priced in.

Current evidence is expected and need not be announced in every brief. Keep the earnings clock in research context, showing event timing only when decision-relevant. Latest-period information is important to near-term catalysts, while the call integrates the whole company's economics, governance, valuation and payoff across the investment horizon.

Prioritise inputs that can change the call, materially change downside or make financing infeasible. An assumption register is not complete merely because each row has a source ID. Record, in the existing structured artifacts:

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Stable assumption ID, metric/period/unit, model version and affected outputs |
| Evidence basis | Reported fact, derived observation, attributed guidance, calibrated estimate, analyst judgment or unavailable input; retain the original observation separately |
| Provenance | Source IDs/locators, publication and first-available dates, scope, access rights and independence from other sources |
| Calibration | Calculation or explicit judgment grounded in relevant history, base rates, operating economics, analogues and source credibility; explain transfers between cohorts, geographies or business perimeters |
| Uncertainty | Plausible range, contradictory evidence, and what remains unidentified; avoid unsupported decimal precision |
| Decision impact | Earnings/cash/target sensitivity, break-even value and whether a plausible alternative changes the call or threatens funding |
| Resolution | Observable falsifier, next evidence needed, review event/date, status and reason for any readiness restriction |

Keep unavailable values null, not zero. A filing supporting an industry trend does not substantiate a specific numerical elasticity or forecast rate. Management targets, compensation hurdles and broker estimates remain attributed inputs, not verified outcomes.

Assess readiness by whether a defensible central estimate and return/risk judgment can be formed. A plausible adverse case reversing the return or call is normal and does not automatically make research incomplete. Explain why the selected case deserves more weight, the price/margin for error, financing survival and what would change the view. An educated estimate can support a call without direct disclosure of every input. Reserve blockers for missing essentials that make valuation or the recommendation arbitrary, rather than uncertainty itself or the absence of a preferred dataset. Do not replace incomplete research with Hold or conceal material risks.

## Operating evidence and competitive advantage

For full company research, follow [competition, capital returns and advantage duration](../../pip-company-model/references/competition-and-capital-returns.md). Map incentives and plausible responses across competitors, customers and suppliers; connect them to demand, pricing, investment and the duration of excess returns. Establish whether additional funded growth creates shareholder value rather than merely increasing reported scale.

Model the economic unit appropriate to the business: customer/cohort, store, order, contract, productive asset or regulated capital base. Reconcile demand, revenue recognition, contribution profit, reinvestment and cash. Use the following distinctions where relevant:

- Wholesale sell-in versus consumer sell-through; surviving-store cohorts versus all stores including closures; channel inventory versus genuine demand.
- Organic versus acquired growth; full-group retention versus selected cohorts; bookings/ARR versus recognized revenue and collected cash.
- Visits/downloads versus paying customers, repeat purchases and profit after acquisition, returns and fulfillment.
- Installed equipment/battery usage versus company shipments; capacity versus utilization, yields and saleable output.
- Consolidated cash versus restricted, subsidiary, customer-funded or regulatory capital that is unavailable for common-share distributions.

For an operating proxy, document population, sample coverage, geography, methodology/revisions, survivor/selection bias and reporting lag. Calibrate it against comparable reported outcomes where observations permit, preserving an out-of-sample period when feasible. If history is inadequate, treat it as directional context or a scenario input rather than a validated forecasting signal. More observations from the same underlying source do not create independent confirmation.

Support a claimed moat with observable retention, pricing, cost advantages, customer economics or returns on incremental investment. Specify how it might erode. A business-model description is not proof of durable advantage.

## Cash, investment and management decisions

Explain large implied remaining-period cash recoveries through receivables, inventory, payables, customer funding, tax and seasonality. Test sustainable cash conversion and liquidity without temporary supplier stretching or unsupported financing. Reconcile prospective dividends/buybacks with distributable cash, required investment, debt and regulatory restrictions.

Use productive-asset commissioning/vintages and remaining lives where material evidence supports them; disclose an aggregate depreciation proxy rather than presenting it as a fully observed asset schedule. Bridge share compensation, vesting, treasury transfers, repurchases, net dilution and cash costs without counting the same economic cost twice. For financial institutions, disclose missing standalone regulatory capital/RWA and liquidity information; a proxy cannot establish regulatory capacity.

Maintain a decision-and-delivery ledger for material management actions: stated purpose and date, economics/incentives, cash committed, expected milestones, subsequent delivery and effects per common share. Questions must distinguish a clarification from a contradiction or an allegation. Public management answers remain attributed claims pending verification. A numerical governance discount does not resolve uncertain cash access.

## Valuation and the return mechanism

Include [market-context and narrative analysis](../../pip-macro-event-analysis/references/market-regime-and-investor-base.md) in full company research alongside operating economics, governance and valuation. Assess relevant macro/sector preferences and credible topical association or reclassification, what is priced in and why a valuation change could persist or fade. Market association can affect price before earnings become material without establishing operating exposure. Keep investor-demand detail proportionate and do not require named buyers or proven inflows; material access/liquidity risks still belong in the decision.

Apply the [probability-versus-payoff distinction](investor-judgment.md#separate-probability-of-success-from-the-payoff-at-the-price). Assess the likelihood of favourable business outcomes separately from the gains/losses available at today's price. A high win rate or earnings beat is insufficient when upside is priced in or infrequent losses dominate. Compare prospective net payoffs, scenario likelihoods/ranges, regime dependence and loss-bearing capacity without requiring artificial probability precision.

For the primary target, retain an executable bridge separating earnings/operating change, multiple change, cash/debt/share-count effects and distributions on a consistent horizon. State the order/convention for interactions. Show an unchanged-multiple or unchanged-discount countercase where meaningful, and solve for the operating outcome or multiple needed to break even at the reference price. Do not count cash/distributions twice.

Support a multiple change with comparable earnings definitions, fiscal/forward horizons, accounting/lease/SBC treatments, business mix and estimate vintages. Use a representative point-in-time historical series when available; label isolated snapshots as sparse references. Do not infer a reliable historical distribution from a few selected dates. If methods disagree materially, identify the economic assumptions causing the difference and explain why the chosen method is appropriate; do not silently select the result that favours the thesis.

Connect the pivotal insight through evidence -> operating/cash effect -> difference from attributed or conditionally price-implied expectations -> credible shareholder return or repricing. Specify actors, incentives and observable conditions. A conditional reverse valuation is not a uniquely observed market belief. A low multiple is not itself evidence of mispricing or a catalyst; a known weakness may justify a persistent discount. An undated possibility cannot substantiate a dated target.

## Challenge and subsequent evaluation

Apply [empirical research and validation](empirical-research-and-validation.md) to case selection, pivotal source verification, calculation review and subsequent outcomes. Maintain coverage/selection records and include failures. Record procedures actually performed separately from plans or structural validation; a populated case library and a demonstrated research track record require actual observations.

Test a coherent joint adverse case where demand, margins, working capital, funding, dilution and valuation can deteriorate together. Distinguish temporary price volatility from permanent impairment, insolvency or value inaccessible to outside shareholders. Explain the selected downside alongside materially lower plausible alternative-method outcomes; a bear target is not a loss floor. Scenario labels do not establish probabilities.

Challenge the most influential assumptions against contradictory primary evidence and a credible competing explanation. Repeating the original model or reading its summary is not independent validation. Preserve unresolved contradictions and classify blockers separately from tolerable uncertainty.

Freeze the information cutoff, model/assumption version and forecast definitions before subsequent outcomes. Match actuals for period, scope and accounting basis; separate operational forecast error, data/perimeter changes, capital allocation and valuation-multiple error. Track management delivery and proxy calibration over repeated observations. An interface for later scoring or a saved watch is not evidence that evaluation or monitoring is running.

Use existing handoff data/artifact references for these records; this standard does not add undeclared envelope fields or install a runtime service. Runtime enforcement and source connections require implemented, tested adapters.
