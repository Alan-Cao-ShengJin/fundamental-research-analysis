# Evidence-driven revision workflow

## Select the update mode

Apply [request routing](../../research-cycle/references/request-routing.md) before the steps below. In a **regular review**, baseline and evidence checks lead directly to valuation/return and decision refresh on the retained operating forecast; reopen model/risk/audit work only for supported changes or invalid dependencies. In a **major earnings/catalyst update**, perform the frozen forecast evaluation below before replacing estimates and recalibrating the model. Both modes reuse unaffected work.

## Establish a reusable baseline

Retrieve the original/latest [conviction record](../../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) along with the model. Preserve its decisive proposition, supporting/contrary evidence, original milestones and any active avoidance conditions. Evaluate new observations against that record in the major-update learning pass; price, forecast and belief changes need separate explanations. Revise a mistaken belief openly rather than substitute another story and label it unchanged.

Take the latest usable components, not simply the latest folder: a study that was never released, or whose review left material findings open, contributes its valid evidence and checked components, while its open findings carry forward as unresolved and its conclusion is not reused. Identify the security/perimeter, model version and executable backend, sources/assumptions, frozen forecast, calculation checks, valuation inputs and prior decision. Preserve the pre-update state before editing; reliable immutable versions or version history can satisfy this without copying the entire research directory.

Reproduce material baseline outputs where possible. Check accounting definitions, model structure, share rights, period mapping and risk assumptions. Reuse unchanged artifacts with their real evidence dates; version changed or explicitly revalidated work rather than stamping every stage as newly completed. Do not retrieve all historical sources again just to refresh timestamps.

If only a narrative report or unrecalculable workbook exists, identify missing calculation/source support. Continue useful source analysis, but do not fabricate an EPS/target bridge. Reconstruct the necessary baseline explicitly, or use [stock initiation](../../stock-initiation/SKILL.md) when a new investment case is needed. Major acquisitions, accounting/perimeter changes, an unusable baseline or a broken thesis can reopen broader stages. Age alone does not require starting over.

## Map evidence to assumptions

Use one table for each changed material assumption: old forecast, new forecast, matched actual where available, and reason/source. Record why a material observation leaves an assumption unchanged when useful.

Distinguish actual-versus-forecast surprise, guidance-versus-aspiration, corrections versus economic change, and newly observed versus already known information. Reconcile units, taxes, scope, orders versus accepted deliveries, firm backlog versus intentions, component versus total revenue and reported versus adjusted profit. Raw transcripts and edited summaries remain separate; an AI edit is not independent confirmation or authority to silently repair numerals. Management explanations are attributed claims, not proven causation.

When actuals replace forecasts, compare on the original basis before rolling the horizon. Keep restated-history errors distinct from what was knowable at the time. Preserve unsupported statements as uncertain or reject them; do not force a model change to match them.

For major updates, compare actuals with the frozen forecast before recalibration. Explain offsetting errors and unresolved causes in the same assumption table; guidance is not an actual outcome. Unobservable event effects remain unscored.

For earnings updates, keep a short inventory of material newly reported flows and the comparable earlier disclosures. When both are cumulative from the same start, derive the latest non-overlapping period by subtraction and reconcile it to the cumulative total. Include cash, operating profit or other flows when they can change the interpretation; do not limit the pass to EPS. Use the saved forecast for the same period where it exists; absence of a matched forecast is not permission to invent one. Identify reversals, restocking, advance collections, tax timing or other explanations with their actual source status. Quantify a contribution before calling it most of a cash result.

Only additive amounts subtract directly. For changing shares, derive incremental attributable profit and use the period's appropriate weighted shares; subtracting cumulative EPS is not generally valid. Ratios need numerator/denominator bridges, balances need stock movements, and changed currency, perimeter or restated history needs an explicit comparable-basis reconciliation. Record unavailability or noncomparability with the relevant sources. For publication, populate `coverage.incremental-flows.candidates` and applicable `incremental-flow` assertions under the [claim controls](../../report-layout/references/claim-support.md); the reader checks that material candidates were not omitted. Keep this analytical inventory out of the brief, which selects only decision-relevant findings.

## Recalculate affected economics and risk

Use shared modeling methods for changed sales, margins, working capital, investment, financing, shares and distributions. Reuse unaffected histories and schedules. Follow changes through dependent statements: investment can materially alter cash/coverage with little immediate EPS effect. A new initiative does not establish its revenue, returns or capital budget; label analyst provisions and test useful ranges.

Review changed financial-quality and governance issues before relying on revised forecasts. Prior reviews can support unaffected matters with scope/date intact. New control/rights threats, restrictions or reporting contradictions can invalidate that reliance. Use stable issue IDs and avoid charging the same loss repeatedly.

Audit affected formulas, statement links, remaining-period implications, funding and preservation of user edits. Reusing a baseline does not mean defending an error in it. Discovering a structural defect requires repair and disclosure before dependent outputs are reused.

## Attribute forecast and valuation changes

For an evidence-only comparison, initially hold quote, FX, earnings year, target date and valuation method/multiple constant. Then give a current investment call on the rolled twelve-month target date and current market inputs; retain the frozen reference clearly when the task is to isolate evidence effects. Show quote, FX, target-date roll, calendar-roll, method or multiple changes separately. Reassess valuation when evidence changes its rationale, without silently moving it or freezing it forever.

Bridge material drivers to attributable profit/EPS, cash after investment, funding/distributions and target/call. Explain offsetting changes. Sequential bridges disclose their order and reconcile; isolated sensitivities are not automatically additive. Preserve price/total return and ordinary/diluted share distinctions.

Challenge the new assumptions and inherited thesis where affected. New evidence may change the call, weaken conviction without changing the label, or leave the case intact. Do not force a revision to make an update appear useful.

## Completion

Retain baseline references, evidence/change records, the revised model or justified no-change result, audit/calculation record, attribution and affected decision versions. A numerical revision includes the [human-auditable Excel model](../../company-model/references/workbook-profile.md), preserving the original workbook and user edits. An evidence-only no-change review can link the existing valid workbook. Reference these through the existing [handoff envelope](../../research-cycle/references/handoffs.md); no new envelope fields or complete new initiation-artifact set is required.

A quantified revision needs a usable baseline, supported changes, recalculated affected dependencies and a consistent scoped conclusion. Missing essentials can leave a useful partial evidence review. Refresh affected saved summaries or mark them stale; preserve unchanged prose and historical artifacts.

## Valuation-only corrections

For valuation-only changes, retain operating forecasts, attribute target changes to method, multiple or earnings-window alignment, and refresh affected decision outputs and current pointers.
