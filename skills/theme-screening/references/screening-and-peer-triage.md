# Fundamental screening and peer triage

Use for a first-pass financial screen, a theme shortlist or a quick comparison before choosing names for full research. A screen generates questions and candidates, not completed stock recommendations.

## Define the comparison before ranking

Record the cutoff, exchanges/share classes, currency, investability constraints, candidate universe and screen criteria. For a theme, map who supplies whom and where demand, bargaining power, margins or a binding constraint could shift. Follow the economics rather than a fixed upstream/midstream/downstream taxonomy. ETF holdings or vendor presets can seed names; verify actual issuer exposure and record omissions, stale constituents and the preset's coverage.

Separate economic competitors, value-chain beneficiaries and return benchmarks. An upstream supplier and its customer can share a catalyst while requiring different valuation metrics. Document material exclusions and keep the screened universe alongside the shortlist so the result can be reproduced rather than reconstructed from winners.

## Pull a small, comparable first pass

Use a relevant existing board, normalized provider response or local snapshot when available before assembling many calls. Inspect its observation dates, field definitions, source coverage and missing values. Follow the [provider guide](../../evidence-intake/references/research-data-providers.md); no particular vendor or CLI is required. A fast screen does not need every endpoint or an integrated forecast for every name.

Choose the few measures that distinguish this universe:

| Lens | Useful observations | Comparability checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valuation | P/E, EV/operating profit, cash yield, book-value or distribution measures suited to the business | Fiscal/estimate year, reported versus adjusted, dilution, currency, leases and debt/cash basis. Loss-making P/E is unavailable rather than a bargain. Banks and insurers require suitable capital/book metrics. |
| Economics and funding | Growth, margin, returns on invested capital, cash conversion, investment needs and leverage | Cycle position, acquisitions, net versus gross claims and whether growth consumes capital. A low multiple can reflect poor returns or impaired earnings. |
| Expectations and change | Dated guidance/earnings estimates, revisions, dispersion and a specific upcoming event | Price-target consensus is not an EPS forecast. Check fiscal period, contributor/vintage coverage, publication time and confirmed versus estimated events. |
| Market context, when useful | Peer-relative returns, breadth, abnormal activity and access/liquidity | Common currency, benchmark and window; corporate actions, source delay and small samples. Price/volume does not directly observe fund flows. |

Keep unknowns as unknowns. Do not reward a company for an unavailable debt balance, missing negative-earnings multiple or a stale estimate. Treat vendor peer groups and cheap/growth presets as candidate generators, not independent verification or investment conclusions. Verify decision-driving anomalies in original disclosures before taking them into a model.

## Select investigations rather than declare winners

Look for a reason to investigate: a valuation difference after normalization, a shift in margins along the chain, a catalyst, changed funding or an unexplained leader/laggard gap. Test a benign or adverse alternative explanation. A supplier constraint only benefits listed shareholders if exposure, pricing, capacity, investment and cash transmission support it.

Keep the full universe available, but return a compact priority shortlist scaled to the request, often three to six names. This is a presentation default, not a coverage cap. For each retained name give the specific reason, decisive uncertainty, next source/calculation and condition for promotion or rejection. Distinguish research priority from expected-return ranking and explain why a prominent excluded name deserves less work now.

Persist the dated screen, definitions, input/source snapshot, chain/peer map, shortlist and next question within the established research package. Reuse existing issuer IDs and dossier links. A subsequent session should read and update those records rather than start from an unqualified new list. Follow the user's requested destination; ordinary authorized local research does not require a new approval for a routine folder choice.

Selected names enter stock initiation or model revision according to whether a valid model already exists. A company recommendation still needs the shared model, quality, governance, valuation and challenge work, including the required human-auditable Excel forecast when forecasts are built or revised.
