| [Howard Marks, The Calculus of Value, 14 August 2025](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/the-calculus-of-value), Interaction of Price and Value | Price can diverge from estimated value for a long time; an investor's ability to endure matters. | Separate thesis from quotation and test staying power, uncertainty and timing. Estimated fair value is a judgment; divergence does not prove the market wrong or justify leverage. |

The main safeguards against confirmation bias here are authored design choices: dated original propositions, meaningful adverse evidence, observable reconsideration conditions, review of funding/shareholder access, and separate eligibility and price-based ratings. No cited study validates this complete workflow, its categories or an AI agent's judgment. The skill has not acquired lived experience by reading these sources.

LLM workflows can produce inconsistent conclusions when the newest prompt or commentary displaces earlier evidence. Retrieve durable records, compare original language with new evidence and explain revisions explicitly. This is a consistency control, not a claim that an LLM feels fear or that a file eliminates bias. Retaining the first opinion without continuing to test it would preserve errors just as effectively as sound judgments.

The review cadence follows the economic observation schedule and material events. There is no imported rule to stop looking at prices, no arbitrary patience period, conviction score, probability threshold, automatic exclusion based on every related-party transaction or mandatory position-sizing formula.
