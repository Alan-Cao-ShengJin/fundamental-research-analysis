# Evidence-based conviction

[Berkshire Hathaway’s 1987 shareholder letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1987.html) distinguishes business progress from quoted prices. [Howard Marks’ The Calculus of Value](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memo/the-calculus-of-value) discusses prolonged price/value divergence. These are practitioner perspectives, not evidence that a particular forecast is correct.

Use a dated, testable proposition, contrary evidence and meaningful break conditions. Retain the original reasoning across reviews so a new commentary cannot silently replace it; revise it when better evidence warrants. Price movements can change prospective returns and financing even when business evidence is intact. The observation schedule and material events determine review timing.
