# Establish and review a conviction record

## Initial record

Use the [blank scaffold](../assets/conviction-record.template.json) when a structured record is useful. Empty arrays and nulls mean not supplied, not verified or passed. Adapt fields to the case without filling them with invented certainty. The record is research support, not a new global handoff schema or an automated decision engine.

Record the security/share class and information cutoff, intended holding/underwriting horizon, primary proposition and positive/avoidance direction. A positive anchor says why economic value can accrue to common owners; an avoidance anchor says why committing capital is unacceptable under an identified continuing condition. A company may have an attractive positive mechanism and a separate controlling exclusion: retain both rather than choosing whichever flatters the desired call.

For the proposition, retain:

- The causal path to model drivers, funded cash/per-share outcomes and shareholder access; which part is observed and which is inferred.
- Evidence locators and dates, source independence, coverage limits, strongest contradiction and best alternative account.
- Current evidence assessment: `supported`, `challenged`, `broken` or `unresolved`, with a reason. These are not ratings, probabilities, positions or ordinal scores. A supported avoidance proposition reinforces an exclusion; it is not bullish.
- Expected operating/conduct milestones, original dates, compatible variation, challenge conditions, thesis breakers and credible conditions for reconsidering an exclusion. Include what can be observed, by whom and when. The current research may already justify an unresolved or broken assessment; do not default to supported.
- Independent constraints that would invalidate the investment despite an intact primary anchor: shareholder access, solvency/funding, essential data reliability and other material risks. Link specialist findings rather than duplicate their accounting.
- Separate investment eligibility (`eligible-for-consideration`, `avoid`, `under-review` or `not-assessed`), valuation/forecast references and the next review/event. Eligibility is neither a price rating nor position permission. A material unresolved exclusion normally prevents new commitment pending the necessary review; ordinary forecast uncertainty does not automatically do so.

Retain the original artifact as an immutable baseline or through reliable version history. Keep the current pointer and reviews separately discoverable. Reuse the issuer's research directory and stable issue IDs; no private facts, company forecasts or alleged misconduct belong in the reusable skill or template.

## Reviews within the governing research modes

**Initiation:** formulate a provisional anchor while investigating the business, then test it against the audited model, governance and counter-case before the final conclusion. Preserve its evidence as of that date. A management-centered or vision-centered view must survive economic and funding checks; a short slogan is insufficient.

**Regular review:** load the old record and valid model, check new evidence, classify what moved, and explain whether the anchor remains supported. Preserve operating forecasts unless concrete evidence supports a change under the revision rules. Price changes update prospective payoff and can change the rating without changing the core proposition. Check a significant or unexplained price move for missing operating, conduct, financing or rights evidence; do not declare it irrelevant merely because it is market data.

**Major earnings/catalyst update:** evaluate the frozen estimates and anchor's expected observations before replacing estimates. Distinguish a noisy period, timing shift, forecast error and a failed core mechanism. Update conviction on the new facts and learning, then recalibrate affected forecasts. A weak numerical estimate can require repair without destroying the business proposition; strong profit cannot cure cash diversion. An event without actuals leaves unobservable outcomes unscored.

Preserve the difference between the intended investment duration, the model's forecast years and the valuation's explicit target date. A plan to invest for at least a year does not imply a stable target, twelve-month price convergence or waiting out a broken thesis. At a milestone or horizon boundary, assess what occurred, funding/runway, return with continued discount, timing/opportunity cost and remaining evidence. Any extension needs a current economic rationale and a new dated decision, keeping the original timing miss visible.

## Decision checks on each review

| Evidence/event | Appropriate conviction treatment | Follow-through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price falls; relevant operating and rights evidence remains sound | Retain the core belief after checking for missing facts | Recompute current payoff and check financing/portfolio constraints; no automatic purchase |
| Price rises far beyond a supported valuation | Core belief may remain supported | Refresh the rating and any proposed exposure separately |
| One delivery miss with a supported temporary cause | Evaluate size, cause, repeated pattern and remaining-period burden | Revise affected forecasts where justified; neither automatic abandonment nor automatic excuse |
| Foundational demand, unit economics, shareholder access or key-person condition fails | Challenge or break the anchor as evidence warrants | Reopen major-update/risk work promptly; do not preserve the call through a replacement story |
| Repeated evidenced minority-value diversion under continuing control | Maintain an active exclusion while the condition persists | Carry governance issue IDs; cheapness, earnings beats and a rising price do not resolve it |
| Allegation or related-party label without established harm | Classify evidence quality and materiality, investigate | No invented finding of guilt; temporary under-review status may be appropriate for a material unresolved threat |
| Verified remediation or changed control/rights | Reassess the avoidance proposition and continuing mechanism | Reconsider only after sufficient relevant evidence; do not require misconduct to remain true forever |
| Missing pivotal data or an overdue milestone | Challenge/unresolved as appropriate | Obtain decisive evidence, bound exposure and revisit the timing; no confirmation from silence |

Record both reasons to retain a view and reasons to revise it. A proposed review status is analyst judgment. The scaffold and structural checks cannot establish source truth or whether a particular company deserves patience.

## Review entry and human communication

Each dated review references the original/latest record, evidence cutoff, observations and their sources, prior/current assessment, change explanation and remaining unknowns. Separate consequences for the core proposition, operating forecast, valuation, price-based rating, eligibility and portfolio action. Link audit/model work when inputs change. A no-change decision needs the evidence checked and reason it still supports the proposition; do not duplicate the whole initiation.

In the investor brief, use a short paragraph: **the reason we remain invested or avoid it; what the latest evidence says; what would change our mind; the next decisive observation.** Do not bury a binding exclusion in the appendix or add a conviction score to imply precision.

## Build judgment over time

At the relevant observation dates, compare original assumptions, model drivers and expected behavior with matched outcomes. Separate correct mechanism from lucky returns, wrong mechanism from temporary mark-to-market loss, premature abandonment from justified exit, and justified exclusions from missed opportunities after real remediation. Include cases that never resolve and correct theses whose payoff failed through price, time or constraints.

Retain counterfactual observations for avoided names when evidence is available, without claiming a missed rally proves the governance thesis wrong. Generalize patterns only over comparable independent cases, noting selection and hindsight bias. Source-specific management delivery history belongs with dated records. Promote a reusable lesson only when its mechanism and limits are clear. A larger archive, confident prose or an LLM's training data does not establish a tested track record.

## Synthetic examples

**A resilient economic mechanism.** A business has documented repeat purchasing, favorable unit cash generation after reinvestment and a funded path to per-share distributions. The anchor concerns durable customer economics and conversion to owner cash, not the share price. A selloff prompts a check for customer loss, collection weakness and funding changes. If the evidence remains supportive, retain the anchor; update price-based payoff. If sustained customer erosion breaks the mechanism, a lower share price does not rescue it.

**A person-centered thesis.** A capital allocator has a dated record of purchasing assets below conservative value, integrating them and increasing distributable cash per share without hidden leverage. The anchor specifies that conduct and the control/capital conditions enabling it. Acquisition volume, charisma or a large founder stake alone is not enough. Succession, changed incentives or a series of economically poor commitments can challenge it before reported profit collapses.

**An active avoidance condition.** Established records show repeated unfavorable transfers to a controller while minority protections remain ineffective. Strong operations and an apparently low earnings multiple leave the exclusion intact. A press statement does not resolve it; an evidenced change in control or enforceable rights, remedy of the exposure and relevant subsequent behavior can justify reopening the review. These examples are invented mechanisms, not issuer allegations or sector-specific benchmarks.
