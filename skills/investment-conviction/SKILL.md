# Investment conviction

State the particular belief that makes this investment worth owning, or the particular reason it remains unacceptable. Preserve that belief across reviews while testing whether its evidence and economic consequences still hold. Conviction means consistency with the evidence and stated principles, not loyalty to the original forecast, target or recommendation.

Use within the governing [research modes](../research-cycle/references/request-routing.md). The default investment horizon is at least one year unless the mandate specifies another. Record the actual intended horizon and milestone dates; a year is neither a promise of market convergence nor a minimum holding period after the case breaks.

## Establish the anchor

1. Identify **one primary decisive proposition** in ordinary language. It can concern business economics, an asset, management, execution or a persistent reason to avoid the company. Explain why losing this proposition would materially change the investment case. Keep secondary assumptions and independent deal breakers visible; the main anchor is not the only fact that matters. If no defensible anchor is established, say so rather than manufacture confidence.
2. Connect it to the foundational drivers in the [company model](../company-model/SKILL.md), funded cash/per-share outcomes and the rights through which outside owners benefit. A named executive requires an observed conduct/allocation record, incentives and key-person conditions. A vision requires feasible funding, execution milestones and evidence of progress. Reputation or charisma alone does not supply the mechanism.
3. State the best dated supporting evidence, strongest contrary evidence, credible alternative explanation and important unknowns. Use [investor judgment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md) and the [challenge skill](../research-challenge/SKILL.md); repeated opinions from the same underlying source are not corroboration. Separate a plausible thesis from demonstrated delivery. Use qualitative evidence strength with reasons, not a conviction score presented as a probability.
4. Specify in advance what ordinary variation is compatible with the proposition, what would challenge it and what would break it. Include expected observation dates, data availability and a next review. Use economically supported ranges where available, without inventing numerical thresholds for a person or business model. Define credible evidence that could change an avoidance view as carefully as a positive view.
5. Freeze a dated conviction record linked to the evidence, issue register, model/forecast, valuation and decision vintages. Read [review protocol](references/review-protocol.md) when creating or reassessing it. The [blank record](assets/conviction-record.template.json) is a reusable scaffold; store completed records with the company's research, keeping original assertions and dated review entries intact. Use existing handoff references rather than a new artifact kind.

## Keep four judgments distinct

| Judgment | Question | What can change it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core belief | Does the decisive economic or avoidance proposition still hold? | Relevant evidence, including discovered errors or stronger explanations |
| Forecast and valuation | What are the supported numbers, uncertainty and attainable value? | Actuals, concrete operating evidence, capital needs, timing and justified valuation inputs |
| Investment eligibility and attractiveness | Would we commit capital, and is today's prospective payoff adequate? | Persistent exclusions, current price, shareholder access, risk and opportunity cost |
| Position or action | What exposure is feasible under the mandate? | Portfolio constraints, funding, liquidity and authorized implementation |

A stable core belief does not freeze model assumptions or value. A good business can become an unattractive purchase after a rally. A cheap stock can remain excluded by evidenced controller conduct. A position can need reducing for real portfolio constraints while the business belief survives. Keep these explanations explicit and route calculations, governance findings, ratings and portfolio choices to their owners.

## Review without drifting or defending an error

Retrieve the original and latest conviction records before reading the newest commentary or rewriting the thesis. Classify the change: price/sentiment, operating delivery, management/conduct, shareholder rights/funding or information quality. Investigate price moves as possible signals of missing evidence; neither dismiss them automatically nor treat a chart as proof that the proposition is false. Price can also affect financing, collateral and dilution, creating real economic feedback.

If relevant evidence still supports the original proposition and no other essential condition has failed, retain the belief with a dated explanation. If material contrary evidence emerges, weaken, suspend or abandon it as warranted even before a full earnings release. Missing or stale essential evidence makes conviction unresolved; silence is not confirmation. Do not wait for the anniversary or the next routine review to address a credible threat.

Preserve the old wording, observations, thresholds and deadlines. Explain changed judgments using new evidence, a discovered analytical error or a better-supported interpretation of existing evidence; otherwise retain the prior evidence assessment and identify the disagreement. Do not move milestones indefinitely, redefine metrics, select friendly sources or replace a failed reason with a new one while claiming the old thesis succeeded. A replacement thesis is separately underwritten and linked to the failed version. Ask whether the same facts at the same price would support the decision without an existing position or public commitment.

## Conviction to avoid

Use [corporate governance](../corporate-governance/SKILL.md) and [financial-quality red flags](../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md) to establish the facts and minority-shareholder mechanism. A supported pattern of value diversion, abusive transactions or unreliable essential disclosures can justify **avoid while this condition persists**, regardless of an apparently low multiple or strong reported operations. Do not average an active exclusion away with growth, an aggregate score or management reassurance.

Retain the specific issue IDs, relevant control/incentives, continuing exposure and evidence required to reconsider. Related-party dealings are not inherently abusive; distinguish normal transactions, allegations, established findings and subsequent remedies. Verify continuity of the controller or mechanism; a name change or new board title does not itself remove the exposure. Credible changes to control, enforceable rights, repayment/remediation and demonstrated conduct can reopen review; a rally, cheapness or apology alone cannot. Neither lifelong condemnation by association nor automatic forgiveness is justified.

## Handoff and ownership

Return the primary anchor and consequence, evidence assessment, contrary case, conditions for retention/reconsideration, any active investment exclusion, review date/triggers and record links. Carry unresolved limitations honestly. For each review give the prior/current belief state, what changed and whether the model, target, price-based rating, eligibility or proposed portfolio action changes independently.

[Investment conclusion](../investment-conclusion/SKILL.md) retains ownership of the target, Buy/Hold/Sell and attractiveness rules. An investment exclusion is a separate **do-not-invest** disposition; it does not mechanically force Sell or authorize shorting. Revise a target that ignores material governance damage; where essentials cannot be valued, keep valuation incomplete rather than inventing a number. If a defensible numerical rating and an exclusion coexist, state both plainly so a mechanical Buy cannot be mistaken for permission to invest. Portfolio sizing and trading remain downstream; conviction never implies averaging down, leverage or an order.

Use matched, dated outcomes, including mistakes and false exclusions, to improve calibration over time. Research context: [evidence-based conviction](references/research-basis.md).
