# Management and issuer questions

Read the evidence pack, latest relevant call/Q&A, prior questions/answers and model sensitivities. Do not ask for facts already available from public filings or connected tools. Narrow partly answered topics to the remaining uncertainty.

Use [current-versus-next-period expectations and peer read-throughs](../pip-company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md) to focus questions. Check what the latest call actually answered, changes in qualified forward language, intervening disclosures and relevant peers that have already reported. Ask what would discriminate among explanations for the next period rather than repeat old questions or treat an analyst/expert statement as management guidance.

Use [investor judgment](../pip-research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md) to select questions that discriminate between explanations and can change the decision. Compare current expectations with earlier promises and delivery; seek operating conditions, constraints or observable milestones rather than reassurance. Carry a reasoned estimate when an exact answer is unavailable and additional questioning has low decision value. Treat public answers according to their specificity, incentives and track record without assuming deception.

Choose questions whose answers can change valuation, cash/liquidity assessment, strategy validity, portfolio risk or another material decision. Do not exclude accounting, balance-sheet or governance issues merely because they are outside the preferred growth narrative. Rank by decision impact and unresolved evidence, not by a fixed question quota.

Write concise, answerable questions. Ask for directions, ranges, comparisons or milestones where exact commercially sensitive numbers are unlikely. Avoid leading management to confirm the model. Keep numerical assumptions and sensitivity calculations in accompanying columns rather than burying several questions in one sentence.

For recurring calls, distinguish recurring operating checks from one-off milestones. State which change is meaningful and how a supported answer updates the model. Consider whether the source/answer is public and appropriate to use; do not seek confidential market-sensitive information as an investing shortcut.

Return a short table: unresolved issue; what is already known; question; current assumption and sensitivity; how different supported answers change the decision; source/check still required. Treat answers as attributed claims to verify, not automatic fact overrides.

Preserve existing user-authored call notes and uncertainty. Replace a workbook tab only within the requested scope and with the [workbook preservation checks](../pip-company-model/references/workbook-profile.md). The host's communication permissions apply: generating this list does not send it to anyone.

Route answers back to evidence intake and the affected forecasts/valuation/portfolio artifacts. If contact is unavailable, research alternative sources or keep the dependent assumption unresolved while other work continues.

Follow the [underwriting standard](../pip-research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) for a decision-and-delivery ledger: link each material question to an assumption ID, break-even/sensitivity, existing management commitment and public verification test. Preserve answer/date/source, follow-up milestone and subsequent delivery. A reassuring answer does not close an issue until evidence supports the model treatment; retain unanswered or untestable items and their readiness effect.
