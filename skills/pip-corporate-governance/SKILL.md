# Corporate governance and shareholder outcomes

Determine who controls the company, whose interests decisions serve, and whether outside shareholders can receive the value the business creates. Feed findings into the model, valuation and investment conclusion; a governance appendix or score alone is insufficient.

For full research, start the ownership/conduct scan during [evidence intake](../pip-evidence-intake/SKILL.md), reconcile capital-allocation findings with the historical statements, and complete the initial decision-driving review before forecasts; deepen material issues during model audit and before valuation. Revisit the same record during thesis validation when testing whether a discount can close. A focused governance request need not build a full company model or issue a stock recommendation.

Treat severe governance threats to minority ownership, rights and receipt of value as high-priority red flags, assessed early rather than left in an appendix. Controller benefit at outsiders' expense, unenforceable claims, destructive related-party transfers or persistent inability to receive value can break an otherwise attractive investment case. Cheapness, growth and a clean audit do not offset that exposure. This priority concerns serious risks, not a fixed weight for governance in every company or a presumption against controlling shareholders.

Share overlapping issues with the [financial-red-flag review](../pip-financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md): governance owns control/rights, beneficiary incentives and value diversion; financial quality owns statement reliability and cash/asset reconciliation. Preserve one root issue and distinguish evidence, severity, confidence and status. Fraud signals are normally a secondary red-flag lens to this governance emphasis, but a material reliability or solvency threat can be independently decisive. Never count the same loss twice.

## Establish scope and evidence

Apply [investor judgment](../pip-research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md) to management credibility. Compare original expectations, revisions, actions and delivery across matched periods; distinguish forecasting conservatism from operating skill and recurring optimism from justified ambition. Consider incentives alongside contrary evidence, without presuming hidden misconduct or attaching a mechanical discount to personality, source category or a single miss.

Confirm the issuer, market, exact share class, economic/voting rights, cutoff and relevant history. Use accessible issuer filings, ownership/insider disclosures, meeting materials, remuneration reports, audited notes, and original exchange, regulator or court records. Adapt sources to the jurisdiction; no particular database, vendor tool or agent architecture is required.

Retain source dates, locators, denominators and reporting scope. Separate beneficial owners from nominees/custodians, votes from economic ownership, shares pledged from the controller's total stake, and announced financing/buybacks from completed transactions. State unavailable coverage. An unsuccessful search means no relevant record was found in the stated sources and period, not that misconduct has never occurred.

## Review the decision-driving issues

| Area | Questions and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership and control | Who ultimately controls votes and cash-flow rights? Map material owners, control chains, dual classes, concert parties, cross-holdings, state/family/sponsor influence and changes. Do outside owners have meaningful voting, exit and distribution rights? |
| Controller and board | Examine the controller's relevant operating/capital-allocation record, board independence in practice, succession, key-person dependence, oversight and management's treatment of minority shareholders. Size, nationality and ownership category are not quality ratings. |
| Conduct and controls | Check material enforcement, restatements, audit qualifications, control failures, related-party dealings, guarantees, diverted funds, insolvency/default history and litigation. Distinguish an allegation, investigation, finding, appeal, remediation and unresolved proceeding; do not infer guilt. |
| Shareholder behaviour and returns | Reconcile insider purchases/sales, pledges, lock-up expiries, shareholder concentration and dividends/buybacks. Consider stated liquidity/diversification reasons and actual net share retirement versus compensation issuance. Selling, pledging or low payout alone does not establish abuse. |
| Financing and capital allocation | Trace issuance, rights offers, convertibles, preferred/debt claims, dilution, discounts, subscribers, acquisitions and proceeds. Compare promised uses and realised returns. Distinguish necessary growth/regulatory capital from persistent value diversion or poor allocation. |
| Incentives and price exposure | Inspect pay/vesting metrics, award terms, insider economic exposure, financing windows, pledge obligations and transactions. Describe evidence-supported incentives and competing explanations; a possible motive is not proof of manipulation or a desired share-price direction. |
| Culture and disclosure | Compare guidance with delivery, corrections, accountability, disclosure consistency and responsiveness to material questions. Corroborate employee/customer or media claims; marketing, ESG scores and IR activity are not evidence of integrity by themselves. |

Use [the assessment rubric](references/scoring-rubric.md) for a comparable qualitative assessment or an explicitly requested numerical score. Do not let a weighted average offset a material minority-rights or integrity concern.

## Connect governance to value and the narrative

Assess [capital-allocation economics](../pip-company-model/references/competition-and-capital-returns.md#test-whether-additional-growth-creates-value) alongside incentives. Compare capital committed, actual purchase prices and subsequent returns/per-share outcomes for acquisitions, expansion, buybacks and distributions. Separate operational growth from value creation and changes in accounting capital from economic improvement; management activity or adjusted EPS growth alone does not prove sound allocation.

Use the [management decision-and-delivery standard](../pip-research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) to trace stated purposes, capital committed, incentives, milestones and subsequent effects per common share. Reconcile buybacks with awards/net dilution, acquisition growth with returns after integration and financing, and reported cash with minority access. Keep clarification requests distinct from allegations; an arbitrary discount cannot substitute for unresolved critical cash-access evidence.

For each material finding, record:

1. **Observation:** dated evidence, status, exposure and affected shareholder claim.
2. **Economic consequence:** potential leakage, dilution, reinvestment returns, payout restrictions, financing cost, cash accessibility or inability to enforce rights. Quantify a supported range, or explain why it cannot be estimated.
3. **Model treatment:** affected semantic metrics/scenarios and which assumptions change. Keep source facts distinct from the judgement applied to them.
4. **Discount persistence:** why an apparent valuation gap could persist. Evidence of investor exclusion or avoidance needs actual ownership/flow data, mandate constraints or attributed investor statements; a low multiple alone does not prove avoidance.
5. **Return path:** who can change the problem, their incentives and authority, an observable event/time horizon, and what would falsify improvement. Test common-share cash returns and per-share growth as well as possible repricing. A promised buyback or possible succession is not a completed catalyst.

Pass this bridge to [valuation](../pip-valuation-thesis/SKILL.md). Estimate common-share value and a realistically attainable target price under the current governance regime, alongside any conditional improved-governance case. Do not assume a peer multiple or full intrinsic value will be reached merely because a stock looks cheap. A justified, persistent discount can reverse a provisional Buy to Sell; credible reform can strengthen a Buy, but requires evidence.

Apply each risk through a stated mechanism. If leakage is already deducted from distributable cash, do not charge it again through an unexplained multiple haircut and discount-rate premium. Where enforcement or cash access cannot be credibly bounded, mark the dependent valuation or Buy case unsupported rather than inventing a precise percentage discount.

## Handoff

Return a `corporate-governance` [handoff](../pip-research-cycle/references/handoffs.md) with ownership/control and rights, dated events, issue-level evidence/confidence, capital-allocation history, the governance-to-value bridge, persistent-discount and improvement cases, affected model/valuation versions, and the few next checks that could change the decision. Reference larger ledgers through the existing envelope.

The summary states whether governance supports shareholder returns, creates a supported discount, or leaves a material issue unresolved, and how that changes the narrative. The final stock call belongs to [investment conclusion](../pip-investment-conclusion/SKILL.md) after model audit, valuation and challenge. No issuer contact, trade, global skill installation or live monitoring is implied.
