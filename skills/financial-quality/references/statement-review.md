# Statement review modes

## Full filing review

Start with the few decision-driving findings, then show the account-level evidence and conclude with implications and unresolved tests. For a comprehensive request, enumerate every disclosed balance-sheet, income-statement and cash-flow line in a coverage ledger. Mark reviewed/no material issue, material finding, not disclosed, or not applicable, with relevant note links. Do not omit a line merely because it has not changed; a stable large obligation can matter.

Expand material findings in the report and keep repetitive no-issue rows in the referenced dataset. For each explain amounts/periods and comparability, business/accounting mechanism, historical/peer context, alternative explanations, cash/financing effects and next verification. A long list of percentage changes is not analysis.

Connect all three statements and notes. Particular investigations can follow profit resilience despite input/FX/freight pressure; inventory and receivable builds; reserve/capitalisation changes; investment income and fair-value gains; FX translation versus transaction effects; acquisition/accounting perimeter changes; or recurring losses despite a turnaround narrative. Select these because evidence makes them material, not as a mandatory suspicion checklist.

## Earnings preview and guidance

Identify preliminary, unaudited, management-guided and filed numbers separately. Preserve ranges, exclusions, accounting definitions and publication vintage. Compare like-for-like periods and any pre-release expectations available at the time. Missing balance-sheet or cash-flow detail stays unavailable; do not manufacture a complete actual three-statement set from a profit preview.

Use disclosed drivers to construct a labelled forecast bridge through the company-model specialist. Distinguish a preliminary result revision from a restated final filing. Revisit the analysis when definitive statements arrive.

## Account-level follow-up and peer comparison

For a follow-up, narrow from statement pattern to the specific account, relevant note/policy, transaction or estimate, then test the explanation across the other statements and cash. Preserve what prior evidence established; expand the disputed mechanism rather than repeating the full report. Separate management's explanation from inference and unresolved possibilities.

For peers, align fiscal periods, accounting standards, reporting perimeter, currency, segment mix, one-offs, leases and adjustments before comparing operating efficiency or growth capacity. A peer ratio difference alone does not prove better accounting or management.

## Financial evidence versus expectations

Consume a current valuation-thesis/model dependency when one exists. Map each priced assumption to financial/operating evidence: what would have to be true, which reported metric can test it, current support/contradiction, required horizon and remaining uncertainty. Revenue growth or a guidance beat is not sufficient to establish an expectations gap or excess return. Price changes can also reflect discount rates, risk premia, liquidity and positioning.

Return a `financial-quality` handoff with coverage-ledger location, observed and derived findings, financial constraints, any expectation-matching results and the next discriminating tests. Conclusions about manipulation require corroboration; preserve ordinary economic/accounting explanations alongside adverse ones.
