# Hardware, devices and server systems

Apply the [shared sector guide](index.md) when selecting the activity and building
its forecast.

## Choose the relevant activity

Separate branded devices, contract manufacturing/assembly, servers, components and
recurring services. High component resale value can inflate sales without similar
economic value added.

## Operating build

Build shipped units by product and channel × net average selling price (ASP),
reconciling to end-customer sell-through and channel stock. Build the bill of materials
(BOM), assembly, freight, warranty and channel incentives without duplicating rebates
already netted in ASP.

Roll active installed base through placements, replacements and retirements; model the
share buying paid services and usage from that base.

## Evidence and calibration

Seek activation/end demand versus shipments, product-level materials and assembly costs,
key supplier pricing, customer concentration, capacity, warranty provisions, inventory
ageing, purchase commitments and cancellation protection. Separate service/software
revenue and cost.

For artificial-intelligence hardware, identify the share of expensive third-party
components and gross versus net recognition.

## Checks and valuation

Test absolute gross profit, cash requirements and returns as well as revenue growth.
Backlog or supplier reservations may reflect duplicated or cancellable demand.

Check whether service growth is consistent with active installed equipment. Use
appropriate cycle cash/earnings and separate recurring economics; do not apply software
valuation to revenue from reselling purchased components.

## Worked example

For 100 devices sold at 20 each, unit materials, assembly, freight and warranty of
12, 4, 1 and 0.5 leave hardware gross profit of 250. A separate active base of 1,000
devices with 40% paid-service adoption and an annual service price of 5 generates 2,000
of service revenue.

All figures are invented. They illustrate selected relationships, not an issuer
forecast or a complete financial model.
