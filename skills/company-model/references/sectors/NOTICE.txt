The English integration is condensed and modified for this research workflow.
Synthetic example figures and the scaffold adapter are independently authored.
No upstream company forecasts, sample reports or scripts are installed as active
research defaults. Fixed output, action, metric-count and valuation quotas are
not adopted. Adaptations do not verify upstream factual claims or source access.

From version 3.0 of this bundle the sector references were rewritten as one card
for each kind of company and extended to kinds the upstream work does not cover.
The cards for those additional kinds are independently authored.
