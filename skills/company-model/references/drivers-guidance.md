# Operating drivers and management guidance

## Operating data

Inventory source trackers, definitions and publication dates. Distinguish production, shipments, retail sales, customs declarations, bookings, recognised revenue and capacity. Match each series to the model segment and its likely timing lag before comparing growth.

Retain raw volume, price and value series with their definitions. A presentation that shows only one series does not replace the broader evidence register.

Gather the breakdowns needed for the [foundational forecast build](foundational-builds.md), including detail discussed only in earnings calls and Q&A. Preserve reported figures, attributed management-only breakdowns, external observations, inferred allocations and analyst estimates as distinct. An absent filing table does not establish that useful finer evidence is unavailable. Keep the important basis and uncertainty beside its model input and retain longer originals in the evidence archive.

Compute monthly/quarterly/annual sums only for compatible series. Where a missing month is derived from YTD, label it derived. Reconcile mutually exclusive, exhaustive categories; when categories overlap or omit a segment, show that limitation. Preserve the original source total and a separate residual rather than forcing one component to balance.

For literal tracker-copy requests, copy faithfully and put reconciliation differences elsewhere. Do not delete or retire existing tabs just because a new Data tab was created; check dependencies and the user's requested scope first.

Compare a meaningful matched pair after results, such as a relevant shipment bucket versus its corresponding revenue segment. Test price/mix, FX and recognition timing before assigning causation to a gap. Missing coverage of a business line stays explicit.

## Guidance register

Apply the [earnings clock and peer read-through procedure](earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md): establish the latest reported period and call and the next forecast period before interpreting guidance, compare current wording with prior comparable statements and subsequent updates, and record a peer read-through as an inference rather than the target's own guidance.

Apply [investor judgment](../../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md): guidance is an interested forecast to assess alongside operating evidence, rather than a model target to copy or uniformly discount. Track original guidance, revisions and matched actuals by metric and regime. Distinguish repeated overpromising, conservative guidance with delivery, execution quality and insufficient history. Adjust assumptions only when the pattern and current conditions warrant it; a beat can reflect an easy target, a miss an external shock. Preserve the issuer's words separately from the analyst's estimate and avoid personality-based judgments.

Read prepared remarks and relevant Q&A plus other notes of the same meeting. Resolve apparent transcription errors against official material; multiple derivative notes do not count as independent evidence. Keep unverified original wording alongside a proposed correction.

For each guidance item record the date, speaker where known, target period, exact qualified statement, whether it is a numerical guide or a directional comment, the source locator and the model assumption it affects.

If YTD already exceeds a full-year guide, check when it was issued, the metric definition and whether it was superseded. Show the implied residual quarter. Do not automatically force the old guide, discard it or extrapolate the latest run-rate; compare plausible explanations and scenarios.

Management's claim that an issue is immaterial is itself evidence, not a command to remove a material accounting, liquidity or downside concern. Preserve relevant caveats. Keep analyst calculations outside the field recording management's own words.

Summarise guidance in decision-relevant language while preserving attribution and qualifiers. Write in the workbook’s existing language (English for a new workbook unless requested otherwise); keep direct quotations in their source language when necessary, with a translation.
