# Investment conclusion

Reach the view after the model, governance, valuation and evidence checks. Follow the [company sequence](../research-cycle/references/company-sequence.md) and [handoff contract](../research-cycle/references/handoffs.md). Read the [stock decision contract](references/decision-contract.md) when forming a recommendation; it defines the required decision, target/return basis and business/thesis explanation.

## Required inputs

Apply the [underwriting standard](../research-cycle/references/underwriting-standard.md) and [investor judgment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md). Qualify ordinary uncertainty; the [decision contract](references/decision-contract.md) defines when an unusable baseline prevents a call.

Load the current model/forecast and assumption versions, [governance findings](../corporate-governance/SKILL.md), model audit, conditional valuation/market-expectations analysis, targeted financial/thesis validation and counter-case. Confirm that governance consequences and material model revisions propagated through valuation and all quoted numbers. Research is an exercise of intelligent guessing with imperfect information: work from what is available and give the call. Do not list missing items in the research brief.

Review the current [red-flag issue dispositions](../financial-quality/references/financial-red-flags.md). High-priority minority-governance concerns and material cash/reporting/solvency risks must change the thesis, model, target or readiness when warranted, rather than survive as decorative risk bullets. Separate suspected issues from established findings. A discounted price does not itself compensate for inaccessible value or an unreliable essential baseline. Follow the decision contract when translating these effects into a target and call.

Apply the [target-underwriting checks](../valuation-thesis/references/target-underwriting.md), including the comparison with consensus, then select a defensible central realisable target under the [decision contract](references/decision-contract.md). Full recognition, partial recognition or no multiple change can each be appropriate.

## Synthesis

Use [investment conviction](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md) to state and preserve the one primary economic or avoidance proposition, its evidence and reconsideration conditions. Read the original record before revising an existing view. Distinguish belief strength, current forecast/value, investment eligibility, the price-based rating and any proposed portfolio action. Bring up red flags only when they should stop investors from considering the stock; if they are not serious, leave them out of the notes. The present mandate plans for at least a year, without promising market convergence or requiring patience after the case breaks.

Form a stock-pitch judgment the reader can repeat in ordinary language: why the price compensates, or fails to compensate, for the risks and how owners earn a return. Prove that judgment with evidence and valuation, rather than substituting technical qualifications for the thesis. Apply the [valuation calendar](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-calendar.md); an incorrect fiscal roll or profit definition requires recomputation before preserving the call.

State the last set of results and next-period expectations ([earnings clock](../company-model/references/earnings-clock-and-peer-readthrough.md)), and any noticeable upcoming catalyst or event that investors should be prepared for going in. Integrate evidence by materiality and explain the pivotal mechanism connecting forecast delivery and shareholder return.

Follow the [probability-versus-payoff assessment](../research-cycle/references/investor-judgment.md#separate-probability-of-success-from-the-payoff-at-the-price): explain whether likely success is already reflected in the price and whether gains compensate for the likelihood and severity of losses. High confidence in business performance is not the same as attractive expected shareholder return. Assess regime dependence and permanent-loss exposure. Support the view with probabilities rather than rough likelihoods: assign probabilities to the scenarios, state their basis and compare the probability-weighted gain with the loss. Do not choose probabilities to obtain a desired recommendation. Keep expected return separate from the primary target; the rating still comes from the primary target.

Explain two things separately, in concise language for an informed nonspecialist, defining company-specific jargon. First, the moat and earnings drivers: how the company makes money and the evidence for its competitive advantage. Second, why one should look at the company as a potential investment: it is cheap, growth is strong and likely to continue, the yield is very attractive, or it is simply not properly valued by the market. If there is no validated overlooked driver, say so rather than inventing a market mistake. Separate supported observations, analyst assumptions and remaining uncertainty.

Select one primary target price for a stated date/horizon and explain the preferred valuation method. Show its signed price return from the timestamped current price and keep bear and bull targets at the same horizon in the analysis, without much emphasis. Show dividend yield, FX and costs separately from the target. State downside mechanisms and observable thesis breakers, checked against our own base case ([investment conviction](../investment-conviction/SKILL.md)); recompute all dependent targets when evidence changes.

Apply the [decision contract](references/decision-contract.md) for the realisable target, rating, price return and separate new-money judgment. Always give a call.

If the target needs a higher P/E, explain why investors would pay it and what evidence would change their assessment. Apply the [rerating test](../valuation-thesis/references/method-selection.md#rerating-and-derating); lower the central target and reconsider the call if the required change lacks support. Saying that a rerating is needed does not justify one.

Understand what type of company you are looking at, dividend, growth or other, and select the valuation method that fits that type ([valuation drivers](../valuation-thesis/references/valuation-driver-and-dividends.md)). Keep that method consistent throughout as the main anchor; other metrics can support it but only as secondary. Recompute the rating, attractiveness and scenarios if the primary method changes.

Test whether governance explains apparent cheapness and whether outside shareholders can realise the value. A persistent justified discount, weak rights or repeated value diversion can overturn a Buy even when headline multiples are low. Equally, do not infer poor governance from price alone or mechanically convert a governance score into a recommendation.

List the important metrics to watch closely, most important first: the ones that could significantly shift earnings against market expectations. Use [research brief](../research-brief/SKILL.md) to communicate the result.

## Boundary and continuation

This is an investment-research conclusion, not an approved position, portfolio weight or order. If implementation is requested, send it to [strategy research (external workflow)](../research-cycle/references/scope-boundary.md) and [portfolio review (external workflow)](../research-cycle/references/scope-boundary.md) with the exact instrument and mandate constraints. An underlying share valuation does not transfer unchanged to a perpetual contract.

Return an investment-conclusion handoff referencing the decision record, Buy/Hold/Sell call, target and return calculations, business/moat and pivotal insight, governance consequences, supporting/counterevidence, catalysts/invalidation and material gaps. Keep prior versions for later learning. Research status and portfolio execution status are separate from the stock rating; a completed Sell need not generate an order, and portfolio decisions require their own assessment.
