# Working with this research bundle

Start at `skills/research-cycle/SKILL.md`. Select initiation, regular review or major earnings/catalyst update before choosing specialist work. Use `skills/catalog.json` and the research coverage plan; account for every bundled skill as performed, validly reused or not applicable according to its requirement. A skill's presence in a plan does not mean its instructions were read or its work was done.

Preserve model-before-valuation ordering, early governance/reporting review, distinct financial-quality passes, industry analysis before operating forecasts and indicator refreshes during reviews, sector-specific operating mechanisms, original forecasts and conviction conditions. Regular reviews retain operating forecasts unless concrete evidence warrants changes. Major updates compare matched prior assumptions with new evidence before replacing estimates and recalibrating. Numerical forecast changes require affected risk/calculation/audit/downstream work and a human-auditable Excel model.

Apply the included decision contract consistently unless the user specifies another mandate. Keep rating, attractiveness, eligibility and portfolio action distinct. An active governance exclusion is not cured by cheapness. English is the default; retain original evidence/locators and translate accurately. Preserve user-authored workbook edits.

Do not add issuer-specific assumptions, private models/reports, live provider responses, credentials or raw imported archives. Portfolio allocation, trading and non-company underwriting remain external. Research and proposed follow-up do not send messages, activate monitoring or authorize execution.

After relevant changes run the structural check, JavaScript tests and Python unittest command in README. Keep names, references, catalog, coverage specification and tests coherent. Keep third-party licenses. Source/metadata checks are not evidence of investment performance or forensic assurance.

For full-report publication, follow `skills/report-layout/references/publication-review.md`: freeze the analysis for fresh writing, review the actual draft PDFs/model/original evidence in a fresh context, record real findings, and publish only the exact reviewed files within the correction budget. `package()` prepares a partial review before release; it does not perform the review. Never use a synthetic review test double or `--legacy` as a current publication shortcut. The host launches contexts; metadata does not prove independence.

An ordinary “initiation on X” or “update view on X” supplies the mandate, not a checklist. Apply the current company sequence, source/state distinctions and feasible-alternative tests yourself. Calculate the model, synchronize the decision record and bound trigger text, render material figures from declared model/source bindings, then freeze and review. Read `skills/report-layout/references/decision-record.md` and `skills/report-layout/references/claim-support.md` for the contracts. Do not impose an automatic forecast haircut, unsourced restoration deadline or identical targets across independent runs.

Apply numerical triggers at their printed precision, separating automatic rating conditions from approximate reassessment levels. Determine financial applicability from the economic claim, not whether a row exists; missing implementation is unfinished verification. Develop operating drivers before detailed valuation, reconcile overall-payoff language with same-horizon eligible cash and price changes, and test matched-period alternatives before causal attribution. Use the owning decision/publication and inference references; these rules do not prescribe a valuation method, sector template or preferred forecast.
